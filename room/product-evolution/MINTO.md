# Governing Thought

The room surface exists to make the user feel their work ecosystem deepening
through Mindrian, because a felt moat is invested in and an invested moat
compounds. The design test for every surface element is one question: does this
make the user feel their venture getting deeper and harder to leave?

## MECE Support

1. The moat is the accumulated venture graph, not the folders. The surface
   exists to make that invisible compounding asset felt, because an unfelt moat
   pulls no one back.
2. The offer loop and room budding are the engine: one suggestion at the right
   moment, picked in one keypress, that grows the room, and the same act of
   growth is both the engagement loop and the moat.
3. The loop is mostly built and unwired, not unbuilt. Highest-leverage fixes:
   kill copy-paste friction (one-keypress selection), fix state freshness, and
   protect reward credibility through calibrated silence.
