WHAT IS NEW IN MINDRIAN, IN PLAIN WORDS
 
The brain behind Larry moved to a faster house. Who cares: every existing Brain API key still works exactly the same, you don't have to do anything. Why: the old Neo4j Aura endpoint is being phased out in favor of a Memgraph-backed one that answers faster and stays in sync better (v1.15.3-beta.38).
Windows: your room stopped freezing after the first save. Who cares: if you are on Windows, every write to your room now sticks, not just the first one. Why: three stacked path-handling bugs, all sharing the same root cause (Windows paths use backslashes and naive string-building chokes on them), found and closed the same day (v1.15.3-beta.40 and beta.42).
 
A scary raw error is gone for good. Who cares: when Larry needed to pause and ask you something, you would sometimes see an ugly raw validation error instead of his normal calm message. Why: this was the 4th time this exact bug reappeared in the codebase, so this time a permanent guardrail was added so it cannot quietly come back a 5th time (v1.15.3-beta.44).
