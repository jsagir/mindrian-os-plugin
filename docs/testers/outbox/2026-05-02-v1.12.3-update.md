## Email body (plain-text version filed verbatim)

Subject: MindrianOS v1.12.3 — Mindrian now remembers where you left off

> Hi all,
>
> Just shipped v1.12.3. The big change in plain English:
>
> Mindrian now picks up where you left off and tailors itself to what you are actually trying to do.
>
> Three things you will feel right away.
>
> 1. LARRY KNOWS WHAT YOU ARE DOING WITHOUT BEING TOLD.
>
> When you are just chatting, Larry stays out of your way. When you are building your room, he files things. When you are running a methodology like Reverse Salient or Beautiful Question, he stays focused on it. No more setup questions every session.
>
> 2. SUGGESTIONS MATCH YOUR ACTUAL JOB.
>
> If you have been working on finding bottlenecks, Larry's next-move suggestions look different than if you have been preparing a pitch. He picks up the signal from how you talk and tunes the menu accordingly. Wrong guess? Run /mos:jtbd set <whatever-you-are-doing> and he will respect that for 24 hours.
>
> 3. ACROSS SESSIONS, ACROSS ROOMS - LARRY REMEMBERS.
>
> Open a fresh terminal tomorrow and Larry will say "you were finding bottlenecks in the customer-acquisition section yesterday - keep going?". If you have been thinking about the same problem in two different ventures, you will see the cross-pollination signal (only on rooms you opt into; sealed rooms stay sealed).
>
> TO PULL THE UPDATE
>
> /plugin marketplace update
> claude plugin update mos@mindrian-marketplace
>
> KICK THE TIRES IN 10 MINUTES
>
> /mos:operator show
> /mos:jtbd show
> /mos:memory list
> /mos:hmi-status
>
> HONEST LIMITATIONS
>
> Most existing /mos: commands have not yet been taught to USE the JTBD signal. Next release.
> The interactive picker still looks like the old style. Phase after this one.
> Cross-room intelligence depends on Brain reachability.
>
> FEEDBACK
> GitHub Issues for defects, jsagir@gmail.com for sensitive feedback.
>
> — Jonathan
