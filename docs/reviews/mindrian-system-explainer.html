MindrianOS is the room that remembers how you got there.

It helps you do better work because it makes your thinking persistent, inspectable, and able to meet its own unfinished business. Theo supplies the method. The intelligence layer helps you navigate the room. The room remains the record.

The idea in one picture

A chat produces a stream. MindrianOS turns selected work into a local room: claims, questions, evidence, contradictions, decisions, and the relationships between them. Nothing becomes true just because a model said it.

The layers cooperate, but they are not interchangeable. Theo does not become the record. The model does not become the decision-maker.

What each layer contributes

01 · MINDRIANOS

The room remembers

Rooms persist even when sessions do not. Filed material survives the chat: claims, notes, questions, decisions, and typed relationships.

02 · THEO

The method gives shape

Theo supplies reusable frameworks and ordered moves. It makes a method reachable and repeatable instead of leaving the next question to chance.

03 · INTELLIGENCE

The intelligence layer helps navigate

It can suggest a next move, surface tensions, and explain what the room contains. Its output is advisory; the local graph and the human gate remain authoritative.

The honest NATO boundary

Built and defensible now

  • Rooms persist; sessions do not have to.
  • Claims land proposed and require a human promotion.
  • Contradictions with filed work surface as typed relations.
  • The room reports unanswered questions and unsupported claims.
  • User material sits beside the frameworks.

B1 and B2: next phase

  • B1: a local verification record and room portrait of checked, disputed, inconclusive, and unchecked work.
  • B2: frame origin and version history, with a filed explanation required to call a change a refinement.
  • The pre-answer reframe gate still needs wiring across the answer paths.

This distinction is the point of the system: design direction stays visibly separate from shipped behavior.

B1 turns a wall of claims into a portrait

The portrait is descriptive. It does not produce a confidence score and it does not turn a source tier into proof.

Stored on the claimwhat was checked, by which method, when, and with what result.
Visible laterthe record remains in the local room instead of disappearing with a session.
Still requires the next slicea user-facing portrait view and the full rung distribution across a body of work.

B2 makes the question itself accountable

When the governing question changes, the room should ask what the previous question got wrong before it shows the new answer.

If the explanation is filed, the change can be treated as a refinement. If it is absent, the room records a relocation and keeps the old work visible. This is the part a patient user with a notebook cannot reliably capture: the reframe is often invisible until two answers diverge.
