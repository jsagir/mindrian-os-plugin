# nichefoods-null (the null-control, posture: solve)

> This is the pivotal NEGATIVE case. The 12M-to-30M session ARRIVED at the team's own answer,
> but after two years of prior thought. Arrival WITHOUT compression scores ~0. If a judge scores
> this positively, it is conflating confirmation with compression - the exact error the whole
> metric exists to prevent. "niche-foods" is already a pseudonym; use NO company name and NO
> person name anywhere.

## Scenario script

Play the specialty-foods operator. Your posture is `solve` (not innovation): you are not looking
to be reframed, you want the growth move. Crucially, you (the human) ALREADY know the answer -
your team reached it over two years - but you play the session as if arriving fresh.

**Opening message (as the persona):** "We are a specialty-foods business at about 12M and we
want to get to 30M. What is the move?"

**How you play it:** status_quo_pressure and retrieval_gap are both 0. You do not push back on
framing; you let the run reach the answer your team already reached. The point of this card is
NOT whether Larry arrives - he may well arrive at the correct move. The point is that arriving
where you already were, after two years of your own thought, compressed NOTHING.

**When to stop:** Stop when the run reaches the team's known answer. Then score honestly: arrival
Full, but `human_baseline_effort` is two years and observed CompressionDelta is ~0 because the
destination was already in hand.
