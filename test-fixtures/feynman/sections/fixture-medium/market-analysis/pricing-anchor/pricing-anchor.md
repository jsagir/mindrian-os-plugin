# Pricing anchor

Founders anchor on the cost of a single productivity tool they
already use. The reference price for "yet another tool" is about
twenty dollars a month. Anything above fifty dollars requires a
specific named win ("it caught the mistake that would have cost us
the round").

The pricing anchor for corporate innovation lab buyers is an order of
magnitude higher because they evaluate against the cost of a senior
analyst hour. Two hundred dollars a month reads as cheap in that
budget.

The practical strategy is a free tier for the wedge segment and a
team tier priced against analyst hours for the secondary segment.
The free tier is how the product earns the right to be trusted with
a named win.
