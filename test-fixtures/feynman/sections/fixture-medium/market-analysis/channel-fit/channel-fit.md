# Channel fit

Solo founders do not respond to cold outbound. They respond to
in-context recommendations from peers and to founder-authored essays
that name their pain exactly. The winning channels are an essay on the
cost of incoherent venture stories, a visible presence in founder
Discord and Slack communities, and a hand-built list of introductions
from accelerator partners.

Paid ads work for productivity tooling in general and do not work for
this category specifically, because the pain is not yet a keyword. No
one searches for "venture story coherence". They search for general
productivity tools and bounce when the tool does not feel different.

The channel strategy is content plus community. Paid comes later,
once the essay has produced named wins worth quoting.
