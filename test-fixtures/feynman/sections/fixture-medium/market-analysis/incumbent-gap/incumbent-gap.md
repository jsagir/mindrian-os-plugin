# Incumbent gap

Notion, Linear, and Obsidian all store venture documents well. None of
them reason across those documents for contradictions. They are filing
cabinets with search. The gap is that none of the incumbents treat the
venture as a near-decomposable hierarchy where a change in one section
can invalidate claims in another section.

Airtable comes closest because of its relational model, but the
relations are user-maintained and no one maintains them. Within a
month the relational graph reflects nothing but the first week of good
intentions.

The gap is not a missing feature. It is a missing stance. No
incumbent treats the venture document set as a live system that
contradicts itself.
