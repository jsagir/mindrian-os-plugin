# TAM estimate

The addressable market for venture innovation tooling sits around four
billion dollars a year when you count seats at accelerators, university
entrepreneurship programs, and corporate innovation labs together.

Bottom-up, the figure is closer to one billion once you remove seats
that already pay for generic project management tools and refuse to buy
a second system. The honest working number is one point two billion,
growing roughly twelve percent a year on the back of corporate
innovation lab expansion.

This is a big enough pond to matter and small enough that a focused
offering does not need to be everything to everyone.
