# Wedge segment

The initial wedge is solo founders who have already paid for one
productivity tool and are frustrated that it does not understand their
venture. They feel the time cost of contradictions but have no language
for it. They describe the pain as "context loss between meetings" or
"nobody remembers what we decided last week".

These users convert on a live demo that shows cross-section cascade
detection in the first minute. They rarely convert on feature
checklists. The wedge is narrow on purpose so the first hundred
customers share a coherent problem and teach the product what matters.

Expansion beyond the wedge is a problem for after the wedge is
defensible, not before.
