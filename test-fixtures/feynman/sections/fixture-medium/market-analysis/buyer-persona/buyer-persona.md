# Buyer persona

The primary buyer is a second-time founder in the first twelve months
of a new venture. They have raised a small pre-seed or are
bootstrapping with runway measured in quarters. They have learned from
round one that the hardest problem is not building the product but
keeping the venture story coherent as it changes.

They are allergic to tools that cost more than fifty dollars a month
until the tool has paid for itself once. They will pay more once they
feel the tool caught a contradiction they would have missed.

Secondary buyers are solo operators inside corporate innovation labs
who face the same coherence problem at a slower pace and with more
budget.
