# Assumption staleness

Every claim in a venture document has an implicit validity window. The
market assumption was true the week it was written. The customer interview
insight was true the month it was gathered. The product spec reflected the
team the quarter it was drafted.

None of these claims come with a clock attached. As weeks pass, each one
quietly goes stale. The founder keeps citing them as if they were still
fresh, and the team keeps reasoning from them as if they were still true.

The fix is to make assumption validity a first-class property of every
claim in the room, tracked automatically, visible at a glance, and
challenged whenever a newer artifact contradicts it. Rejection of a
challenge is itself data that improves the next scan.
