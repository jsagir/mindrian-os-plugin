# Hidden cascade failures

When a founder updates one part of the venture story, the related parts
rarely update with it. A new pricing insight lands in the business model
doc, but the financial projections still reflect last month's
assumptions. The competitive analysis still names a competitor who
pivoted away two weeks ago.

These silent cascades are the real killers. Each one on its own looks
small. Together they produce a venture narrative that contradicts itself
in three places by the time it reaches a serious investor, and no single
person on the team can see all three contradictions at once.

The cost is not intellectual. It is temporal. Every contradiction
discovered late costs the founder a week of meetings to repair trust,
plus the week that produced the contradiction in the first place.
