Hi,

/m:os beta.65 is out. This update helps you see what your research actually covered, and look beyond the familiar language of your own field.

Here’s what’s new:
  • Clearer research trails. Every run now states what it searched, what it refused, and what it did not run.
  • A better starting point. Deep research opens with a scan of what is already deployed, the field’s own terminology, and the recent state of the art.
  • A wider search. Searches are built around practices, mechanisms, and neighboring fields, not just the words you used to describe your problem.
Update or install /m:os

Already installed? In Claude Code, run:
/plugin marketplace update

Then, in your terminal:
claude plugin update mos@mindrian-marketplace

New here? Run in your terminal:
npx @mindrian_os/cli
/m:os is a plugin for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, and Cowork, built on the PWS (Problems Worth Solving) method. It gives a hard problem a data room on your machine, where evidence and assumptions stay separate and you decide what to test next.
