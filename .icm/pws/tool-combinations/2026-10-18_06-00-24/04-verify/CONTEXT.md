# 04-verify

## Inputs
Owner request and existing PWS source artifacts.

## Process
Check source hashes and spans, the contract/schema, grounded versus proposed assertions, exact existing tool targets, machine/human/face consistency and refusal cases. Verify existing toolbox integrity, protected work and published paths. Record validation and telemetry limits.

## Outputs
Stage report and evidence.

## After Output
Call icm.sh stage-done pws/tool-combinations --stage 04-verify.
