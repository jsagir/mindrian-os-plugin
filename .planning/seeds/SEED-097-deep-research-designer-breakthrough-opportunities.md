# SEED-097: Deep Research Designer for breakthrough opportunities, built with the MCP intelligence services

> **Promoted to Phase 363, 2026-09-29, with a navigator reframe.** The navigator restated the
> original intent: "the initial intent of it was building it as a deep research planner. deep
> and quick runs." Phase 363 is framed as a Deep Research Planner with two run modes (quick:
> one bounded pass; deep: multi-step planned research with iteration and synthesis). Everything
> below (MOS-CANVAS perspectives, approval policy, the Whitespace + OpenAlex first slice) feeds
> that planner and is subordinate to it. See the Phase 363 entry in ROADMAP.md.

The critical next phase. A research designer that turns the room's own signals into deliberate,
literature-scale research, so the breakthrough engine stops being limited to what is already in
the room. It is the MCP-based intelligence layer the navigator asked for at the start of Phase 355
(the pptx "Algorithmic Generation of Solutions", April 2025, and the algorithm-incorporation
devpkg zip), which 355 and 355.1 deliberately narrowed to room-local honesty groundwork (Home A).

## Discuss-phase brief: the room reaches outside itself

**Governing thought:** the room should reach outside itself, and the graph should decide when,
which lens, and what query. The research designer is the owner of that decision. MOS-CANVAS
perspectives remain independent strategic pathways: each can produce a useful local finding, but
the designer decides whether that finding warrants an external research child run, shapes the
query, applies the approval policy, and returns the evidence to the originating card.

**First slice:** prove **Whitespace + OpenAlex** end to end. Start with a two-section room cohort,
ask whether a scoped gap is absent from the literature or only absent from the room, compose one
bounded query family, obtain the approved corpus response, and return a hash-anchored evidence
card. The existing OpenAlex adapter makes this the lowest-risk test. It has a clean falsifier when
the gap is covered under another term, irrelevant, or caused by extraction failure. It avoids the
unsettled HSI sign convention and the unverified Scientific Roadmapping Theo seam (checked
2026-09-27: the content is in the graph, the typed seam cannot read it; see Live Theo check).

**First discuss decision:** choose the external-research approval mode: per-query exact-string
approval or a standing audited policy for ambient runs. This is a product behavior decision. A
policy that requires a human turn for every query prevents the room from initiating useful runs;
any standing policy must still bind provider, scope, query family and audit record.

Everything below is the evidence, review history, contracts and appendix for that brief. The
details remain authoritative, but they must serve this first proof rather than obscure it.

## Governing MOS-CANVAS briefing: independent strategic perspectives

**Each MOS-CANVAS part must work independently.** RS, HSI, Whitespace, Eureka,
Find Analogies, and Scientific Roadmapping each ask a different strategic
question. Each may start from a navigator goal or relevant room signal, produce
its own evidence-backed finding, request research when warranted, and reach
human review and proposed filing. No finding needs agreement from another
part, all three of the PWS author's signals, or a five-engine run to count.

MOS-CANVAS provides common provenance, privacy and approval boundaries, a
research gateway, and a portfolio view. The portfolio may link related
findings, compare perspectives, or investigate disagreement. It must preserve
the original question, evidence, uncertainty, ranking logic, and standalone
card for each part. A shared contract is interoperability, not one combined
method or one universal score. **This rule governs every table and pipeline
below wherever older wording could imply mandatory convergence.**

### What makes each pathway strategically different

These are intended product contracts, not claims that every current producer
already implements its full pathway. Shared retrieval code is acceptable;
changing a label on the same question, query and output is not differentiation.

| Perspective | Strategic question and standalone output | Research path and evidence needed | What would defeat the finding? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reverse Salient (RS) | What lagging component is holding this system back? A bottleneck claim with dependencies, affected outcome and intervention. | Trace dependencies and performance; search interventions and failure reports for that specific bottleneck. | Improving the component does not change the outcome, or another dependency dominates. A text differential alone cannot establish a bottleneck. |
| HSI | Where do vocabulary and underlying meaning diverge? A candidate meaning bridge or term collision with both readings explained. | Compare definitions, uses and mechanisms across domains; seek evidence that the apparent similarity or difference is real. | Context removes the divergence, or there is no shared mechanism. Preserve `neither`/`unresolved` when the two direction labels do not fit. |
| Whitespace | What relevant relation, capability or question is absent from the examined coverage? A scoped gap and coverage record. | Compare the room or a bounded corpus against an explicit reference set; search synonyms, prior attempts and reasons for absence. | The gap is covered under another term, irrelevant to the goal, or caused by extraction/retrieval failure. Room absence is not field-wide absence. |
| Find Analogies | What mechanism from another domain could address this function? A source-to-target mechanism mapping and transfer conditions. | Search by function, causal role and operating conditions; examine both supporting examples and transfer failures. | A required condition does not hold in the target, or the connection is surface resemblance without a causal mapping. |
| Eureka | Which opportunities in the permitted material deserve exploration now? A ranked portfolio of candidate options with reasons, novelty-to-room and next actions. | Harvest and retrieve candidates from its own substrate; investigate the highest-value unknowns in selected options using its own portfolio logic. | Candidates are known duplicates, lack evidence or relevance, or the ordering adds no value. Other perspectives may contribute, but their execution is not required. |
| Scientific Roadmapping through Theo | Which apparently fixed constraint blocks a quantified ambition? A path map, constraint ledger, falsifiable project questions and downstream unlocks. | Enumerate routes; examine empirical or derived bounds; seek assumption-breaking evidence and discriminating experiments. | The bound is established, the proposed test cannot discriminate, or the unlock chain depends on unsupported additional breakthroughs. |

A **perspective** chooses the strategic question and required output. A hat or
persona changes how that question is challenged; a discovery pattern describes
the relationship; a source lens chooses where to look; a provider retrieves
evidence. These are separate roles. Two perspectives may legitimately share
the same provider, passage, Jev policy or detector without becoming the same
research pathway. Each must expose its distinctive question, evidence standard,
counterevidence search and next decision.

## Built alongside the MCP intelligence layer, not after it (navigator, 2026-09-27)

The research designer and the MCP intelligence services are ONE critical phase, designed and
shipped together. Neither comes first. Each perspective can use only the services
its question needs; the designer is a consumer and a producer on their shared service layer:

| Shared capability (local services and governed MCP seams) | What the research designer does with it |
|---|---|
| Engine services: RS, HSI, whitespace, eureka as typed MCP tools | Consumes their findings as query candidates (driver 2) |
| Lens service: hats / persona lenses | Asks it WHAT is worth researching for a finding (driver 1) |
| Pattern service: problem-type rung, discovery-pattern taxonomy, Terminology Translation, Problem Decomposition | Shapes each query's language and splits the question (driver 3) |
| Query composer + egress audit (Part 8) | The designer's own output: audited query strings awaiting navigator approval |
| Corpus service (OpenAlex first; arXiv / PubMed / patents; optional Semantic Scholar / Scopus) behind the Theo seam | Runs the approved queries; returns reviewed, hash-anchored rows |
| Judgment service: Jev through Theo as keyholder (Theo SEED-015) | Types each result (supports / contradicts / says nothing; pattern id; novelty) |
| Verification service: guarded `find_connections` on the shim | Stamps each research-backed pairing on every surface |

Design rule: the 355 BRIEF's restricted enum/bucket/registry-ID shape governs
Theo analytical and judgment requests. Exact public search queries need their
own audited approval contract. The common local result envelope never crosses
either boundary, and private room content accompanies neither request.
A perspective supplies the designer with a strategic question, candidate and evidence needs.
The research designer owns the cross-service research loop: it decides when external research is
warranted, selects the applicable lens and pattern, composes the query family, and manages the
approved child run. When external research is used, the exact query is audited and approved before
fetching; the resulting evidence returns to that perspective's card. Discuss-phase scopes
the service contracts and the independent perspective paths together, so each
service has a concrete consumer without forcing every consumer through one chain.

## Each research runs on a cohort of context from across the room (navigator, 2026-09-27)

A research question is never framed from one finding alone. The designer assembles, per
research, a **context cohort**: the insights from DIFFERENT parts of the room that bear on the
question, read together. For example, an HSI pairing between two solution-design artifacts gets
its cohort from problem-definition (the governing question), market-analysis (who would care),
open questions and unsupported claims (the gaps), CONTRADICTS edges (the tensions), prior
REJECTED_BECAUSE decisions (what the navigator already ruled out) and the stage. The cohort is what
lets the lens and the pattern services ask the RIGHT question, and it is what makes a research
result land as a room-wide insight instead of a pair-local one.

Rules for the cohort:
- **Local graph first.** Built only from the room's local SQLite graph (`room/.room-graph/`
  room.db) through `lib/core/navigation.cjs` reads, the single chokepoint: graph neighborhoods,
  `room-delta-facts`, open questions, unsupported claims, contradictions, decision nodes. Reuse
  `context_assemble` (its four legs) and the navigation readers before writing anything new.
- **Cross-section by design.** A cohort must span at least two ICM sections (not two artifacts in
  one folder), chosen by graph distance and section role, not by keyword match. Its composition
  (which sections, which node types, how many) is recorded with every research run so a result can
  be traced back to the cohort that framed it.
  Independence concerns other perspectives, not the context needed for a claim.
  A goal-only or cold-room start may produce a provisional question/path artifact,
  with `context_insufficient` and the missing context named. It must not pretend
  to satisfy this research-cohort rule or fabricate a second section. External
  research under an exception to this rule is an explicit discuss-phase decision.
- **Never egresses (Part 8).** The cohort stays on the machine. Only the typed query the navigator
  approves crosses; the cohort shapes the query, it is never sent with it.
- **Results come home to the right sections.** A full research run files under `research/`;
  its proposed insight links to the relevant cohort sections through navigation-backed typed
  edges. Only a human-ratified claim is promoted, so the next ambient run can see the result
  as room delta without copying the full source bundle across folders.

## Why This Matters

Today (after 355 and 355.1) every breakthrough finding is a connection between two things
ALREADY INSIDE the room, checked against the methodology graph. Nothing reaches outside: no web
call, no literature corpus (verified 2026-09-27: zero Tavily / WebSearch / Semantic Scholar /
arXiv / Scopus / http references in the ambient and stamp code). The origin deck's whole point was
the opposite: two encoders over thousands of papers per domain, where the unusual pairing hides.
The room can only surprise the user with what the user already wrote down. The research designer
closes that gap, and it is the moat move: WHEN to research, WHICH lens, WHAT query, in WHAT
sequence, decided by the graph and the engines, not by the user typing a search.

## The design shape (navigator's words, 2026-09-27)

Available strategic inputs, chosen by the question rather than run in a fixed sequence:

1. **Research driven by hats.** The Six Thinking Hats / persona lenses decide WHAT is worth
   researching (the white hat asks for the missing facts, the black hat for the prior failures and
   "someone tried this last year", the green hat for the adjacent domains, the yellow hat for the
   value case). Reuse before build: `/mos:think-hats`, `/mos:persona`, `agents/persona-analyst.md`.
2. **Queries driven by the engines.** RS (reverse salients), HSI (the lexical-vs-semantic
   differential), whitespace (structural holes) and eureka findings generate the query
   candidates. An engine finding is a question the corpus should answer, not only a card.
3. **Problem-pattern driven.** The problem type rung (undefined / ill-defined / well-defined /
   wicked, never keyword-inferred, per 355.1 AMB-07), the discovery pattern taxonomy (devpkg #3:
   analogical transfer, constraint relaxation, structural isomorphism, mechanism bridging,
   recombination, scale translation, temporal translation, negation insight), Terminology
   Translation (devpkg #1: "binding constraint" also searches "reverse salient", "bottleneck",
   "single point of failure") and Problem Decomposition (devpkg #7: 3-5 sub-questions, unknowables
   flagged as whitespace) shape the query language.
4. **Constraint-driven through Theo.** Scientific Roadmapping starts from a
   valuable but disputed goal, enumerates paths and their limiters, and generates
   testable ideas from constraints that may be assumed rather than fundamental.
   It can operate without an upstream engine finding or a hats session.

## The PWS author's three-signal briefing: Jev-led interpretation (navigator, 2026-09-27)

The PWS author's algorithm R&D briefing proposes that whitespace, novelty, and a cross-domain
connection converging on one finding make a breakthrough candidate. Its own caveat is
binding: this is a **thesis, not a demonstrated capability**; the surveyed algorithms
were not run as a combined system. Use them as inspiration for Jev policies and for
evaluation, not as a mandate to install five algorithm packages or recreate the old
Mindrian V2 pipeline. The evidence review is
`docs/SEED-097-PWS-AUTHOR-ALGORITHM-REVIEW.md`.

Source-check corrections that must survive planning: the cited gap-opening result is
an **odds ratio of 1.58**, not a 58 percentage-point increase; AutoDiscovery's
reported **67%** is the share of system-surprising discoveries that also surprised
experts, not a correlation coefficient. The briefing's BisoNets bridge formula is
its own proxy, not a validated algorithm. Its topic uniqueness threshold, runtime
and five-week implementation estimate are unmeasured Mindrian assumptions.

The five named inspirations and their intended Jev questions are:

| Inspiration | Jev judgment over a locally nominated candidate | What stays outside Jev |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent homology (GUDHI/Ripser) | Is this proposed missing relation meaningful, already covered, or an extraction artifact? | Graph construction, actual topology, counts and persistence. |
| BERTopic + Bidirectional Topic Matching | Is a reference-corpus theme absent, present under another name, or irrelevant? | Corpus collection, coverage, topic retrieval and absence evidence. |
| AutoDiscovery Bayesian surprise | Is the finding a restatement, expected extension, non-obvious transfer, or unsupported? | Bayesian prior/posterior math; the existing room-centroid cosine-shift diagnostic is a different metric. |
| Uzzi atypicality (Novelpy) | Does an unusual literature pairing carry a real transferable mechanism? | Co-citation frequencies, null models and z-scores, if ever needed. |
| BisoNets/bisociation | Does the proposed bridge connect distinct domains through a transferable structural role? | Candidate discovery and graph traversal. |

Jev is the **semantic judgment layer**: fixed, stated, versioned closed-enum policies,
called through Theo's proposed keyholder seam after its own gate is met. The local
MOS-CANVAS still gathers cohorts and candidates, keeps exact provenance, performs
deterministic counts and ranking, audits query egress, and records human decisions.
Jev cannot infer a whole corpus gap or a graph's topology from a small typed state,
and its Choice/Noul outputs are not interchangeable numeric measurements. Low
confidence or missing evidence remains `uncertain`/`unmeasured`, never a fabricated
zero. This follows the existing `355-BRIEF.md` per-capability Jev design.
Before a policy is treated as useful, test whether the Part-8-safe handles, enums and
buckets actually give Jev enough information to make that judgment. Room prose must
not be quietly added to the Theo request to rescue a weak result; if the typed state
is insufficient, keep that judgment local or mark it unresolved.

Phase 355 establishes no general Jev-readiness claim. Its HSI thinking-mode policy
was **not_adopted**. The citation calibration had no `supports` gold examples,
all eight contradictions were constructed, and gold was machine-labeled. The
usefulness trial supplied synthetic free-text excerpts, not the restricted
production wire projection. Each new policy therefore needs held-out evaluation
using exactly its allowed runtime state, including insufficient evidence and
abstention cases. Track measured accuracy, coverage and calibration separately
from model confidence. Framework-path status, source-to-claim support and human
ratification are separate evidence fields.

For every candidate, expose the applicability and measurement state of the three
evidence dimensions (whitespace, novelty, cross-domain connection), without
requiring all three to be present. A Jev reconciliation verdict is useful when
perspectives disagree. A three-way conjunction is one optional portfolio policy;
it must not suppress strong one-perspective discoveries. Compare it against
each perspective's standalone yield on frozen cases. Temporal Convergence,
Terminology Translation, and
Discovery Pattern Taxonomy are already in this seed; evaluate their Jev policies
within the same loop rather than listing them as newly missing capabilities.

**Show your work:** every card exposes its perspective, strategic question,
local evidence/coverage, limits and review state. Applicable attachments expose
graph paths, detector signals, closest prior/counterexample, source support,
approved query/provider/scope and hash, result citations/hashes, and evaluated
Jev policy/version and abstention. A local gap need not invent an external query;
a roadmap need not invent pair endpoints. Record who reviewed which returned
sources. A provider response is retrieved evidence, not an already-reviewed claim.

**Pilot bar:** at least 5 of the top 10 cards judged interesting by a stated,
blind human rubric, as in the PWS author's briefing, with a separate relevance set and
diagnostic metrics for each perspective. If fewer than ten eligible cards exist,
report the actual denominator and treat the top-10 bar as unevaluated. Include
appropriate recall/coverage, evidence completeness, cost and a non-regression
comparison where the baseline is comparable. Phase 355's 43/96 covers synthetic
HSI/RS pairs, with no Eureka output; it is neither a top-10 nor a six-perspective
baseline.

## Shared layer and minimum implementation changes

The shared layer owns context access, provenance, run/status records, approved
retrieval, applicable checks, review and filing. The perspective owns its
question, candidate selection, evidence requirements, query plan, stopping rule,
ranking and explanation. A small adapter joins them. The six perspectives do
not each get a new fetcher, scheduler, cache, database, approval flow or UI.

| Shared responsibility | Existing home to reuse | Bounded extension for SEED-097 |
|---|---|---|
| Room/context access | `navigation.cjs`, `navigation.getResearchPreflight`, `research-context-extractor.cjs`, `context_assemble` | Record cohort section coverage and evidence refs; expose the selected perspective's context needs. |
| Execution entry and status | Existing command/MCP routes, `ambient-run.cjs` adapter seams, `recovery/stage-envelope.cjs` | Explicit perspective selection and standalone outcome handling; retain `ok`, `empty_valid`, `degraded`, `failed`, and `blocked`. |
| Evidence request | `rs-query-matrix.cjs`, `eureka/online-pattern-query.cjs`, existing research/lens flow | Common request envelope with perspective/question IDs, purpose, bounded scope, counterevidence query, source plan and stop/budget rule; local graph translation remains local. |

| Verification and judgment | `verification-stamp.cjs`, governed `brain-client.cjs`, measured Jev policies via Theo | Invoke only applicable checks. Canon path evidence is one evidence kind; a roadmap bound needs its own evidence. Keep calibration distinct from model confidence. |

Reuse those functions where their contracts fit, but introduce a bounded branch

## SEED-097 decision: MOS-CANVAS

The opportunity-extraction layer is named **MOS-CANVAS**. Find Analogies,
Whitespace, Reverse Salient (RS), HSI, Eureka, and future detectors are all MOS-CANVAS
parts. Each is an **independent strategic perspective** with its own question,
evidence, candidate, ranking logic and useful standalone output. The shared
candidate ledger, verification path, research gateway and human-ratification
rules make their outputs comparable and linkable when a finding warrants it.

MOS-CANVAS is not a replacement algorithm and not a sixth engine. It is the governed
canvas on which each perspective can work alone or in a chosen combination.
A valid single-perspective finding can become a local proposal, a cross-room seed,
or an approved external research query without waiting for another engine.

### MOS-CANVAS strategic perspectives

- Find Analogies: structural/function transfer.
- Whitespace: missing links, absent capabilities, and unexplored regions.
- RS: reverse-salient bottlenecks and asymmetric meaning.
- HSI: semantic/lexical divergence.
- Eureka: retrieval, embedding similarity, and opportunity harvesting.
- Scientific Roadmapping through Theo: a constraint-first path from a domain
  ambition to testable ideas and catalytic unlocks; it can run from a user goal
  without an RS, HSI or whitespace nomination.

### Independent operation and optional synthesis

MOS-CANVAS gives each perspective a small common **local** record around its
method-specific output. Proposed envelope: `schema_version`, `candidate_id`,
`run_id`, `perspective_id`, `question_ref`, `cohort_ref`, `result_kind`,
`payload_ref`, `evidence_refs`, `coverage`, `stage_status`, `review_status`,
and method/policy versions. All refs resolve locally; this record is never a
Theo request. Extend existing records and stage envelopes before creating a
second registry, store or run ledger.

`result_kind` selects a validated payload: bottleneck/dependencies, semantic
pair, scoped gap, mechanism mapping, portfolio option, or roadmap/path/constraint
record. Pair endpoints and direction apply only to pair results. Rung, metric,
limiter and unlock chain apply to roadmapping results. Query requests, ranking,
novelty assessment and Jev verdicts are optional attachments, with their own
provenance. `not_applicable`, `unmeasured`, and a performed-but-unverified check
remain distinct. Never manufacture a pair, score, constraint or remote call
just to fill common fields.

Optional synthesis example:

```text
Find Analogies finds a transferable mechanism
        + Whitespace finds no equivalent in the target domain
        + RS identifies the target bottleneck
        + HSI detects word/meaning divergence
        + Eureka retrieves a supporting analogue
        ↓
linked MOS-CANVAS candidates with a shared opportunity hypothesis
```

The navigator may see one perspective's card or a portfolio view that links
related cards. The system deduplicates genuinely repeated claims but does not
collapse different strategic interpretations of the same material. Agreement
is evidence, not automatic truth; disagreement is itself a research lead.
No conjunction of engines is required for a card to be shown or ratified.

### Per-perspective path and optional portfolio path

```text
navigation-backed context cohort or explicit goal
      ↓ one selected strategic perspective
perspective-specific candidate and evidence
      ↓ applicable verification / Jev judgment
perspective-specific ranking and human review
      ↓ optional cross-perspective comparison or synthesis
local claim, cross-room seed, or approved research query
```

The AHP/tail-quadrant method belongs to Eureka's portfolio question; it is
not a universal ranker for every perspective. A shared portfolio view may
compare calibrated human usefulness and evidence completeness, while retaining
the original within-perspective ordering and reasons.

Do not force all engines into one embedding dimension. Preserve raw vectors and project
to shared scalar dimensions: semantic similarity, structural similarity, divergence,
novelty, cross-engine agreement, verification confidence, and human usefulness. This
allows 384-, 768-, and 1024-dimensional substrates to coexist while the ranker consumes
calibrated comparable features where comparison is justified. The July consolidation
verdict was demoted in `355-BRIEF.md` section 5 to a comparability requirement:
consistent sign semantics, disclosed floors and meaningful differential comparisons.
SEED-097 does not require a new encoder, forced re-embedding or index migration.
Any later consolidation needs evidence of benefit and its own bounded change decision.

### MOS-CANVAS run state

```text
DRAFT → CONTEXT_READY → PERSPECTIVE_RUN → RESULT_READY
  ├─ sufficient local evidence → REVIEW_PENDING → FILED_PROPOSED
  └─ evidence request → AUDITED → APPROVAL_PENDING → APPROVED
       → FETCHING → NORMALIZED → RESULT_READY

FILED_PROPOSED → human gate → CONFIRMED / REJECTED / DEFERRED
```

These are logical transitions to map onto existing run, stage and opportunity
records during planning, not a mandate for a new database state machine. Saving
a research artifact or proposed finding is distinct from confirming a truth
claim. A rejected, deferred, empty or failed run keeps its evidence and reason.
Local-only completion never waits for a corpus query or a Jev call.

No **external-corpus** adapter is callable before `APPROVED`; approval binds to
the exact query hash. Generic methodology reads use their existing governed
read path and do not require external-search approval.
Room content never crosses the boundary. Cross-room transfers contain only structural
handles, enums, quantized scores, and hash prefixes. The receiving room assembles its
own cohort through navigation.

### MOS-CANVAS vertical slice for SEED-097

```text
two-section local cohort
→ Whitespace perspective and scoped gap candidate
→ optional hat/persona lens where it sharpens the question
→ one pattern-shaped query family
→ research designer approval-policy decision and exact query audit
→ one corpus adapter behind Theo

→ proposed navigation filing

```

Run a second perspective on the same case only to test whether it contributes
new evidence or a materially different interpretation. A full five-part run
is a separate portfolio experiment, not the minimum viable workflow.

The research-builder use is: a detector nominates a path; Scientific Roadmapping

### Acceptance evidence and gates

- **All six perspectives:** invoke each with all other perspective adapters
  disabled. Each produces its applicable reviewable artifact and filing proposal,
  or an honest typed insufficiency on a fixture that lacks required inputs.
  Each also needs a positive fixture proving its substantive path works; six
  refusals do not constitute completion. A one-perspective pilot is sequencing only.
- **Differentiation:** on a shared case, reviewers can identify each perspective's
  question, evidence requirements, research behavior, output and falsifier.
  Identical conclusions are allowed; different wording or forced disagreement
  does not count as strategic differentiation.
- **Shared-layer reuse:** all six consume the same context, evidence/status,
  approved retrieval and proposed filing services where applicable. Record each
  changed production seam and the gap it closes. No per-perspective provider,
  cache, approval ledger or parallel room graph is added.
- **Branching and failure:** local results complete without corpus approval or a
  Jev call. Non-pair roadmap/gap results survive rendering, filing and replay.
  Failed retrieval, valid empty results, unmeasured evidence and not-applicable
  checks remain distinct. Unresolved framework handles alone do not suppress
  findings from an explicit perspective's review queue.
- **Method-specific quality:** RS requires bottleneck/dependency evidence; HSI
  measures direction accuracy with a `neither fits` outcome; whitespace measures
  coverage and false absence; analogy measures mechanism-transfer validity;
  Eureka measures opportunity usefulness/ranking; roadmapping measures limiter
  classification, discriminating tests and defensible unlocks. Planted-transfer
  recall applies to transfer tasks, not as the only quality measure for all six.
