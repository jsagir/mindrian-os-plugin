# The execution-truth program

## Why a program and not six seeds

Seven seeds and three pasted documents were filed in thirty hours. They describe one disease
from four chairs: silent degradation under a plausible success surface. The brief already carries
the right decomposition (code, harness, AI behavior, UI) and the right order (execution truth
first, transitions second, inspectability third, contracts fourth). This file maps every filed
item onto that order so a planner opens one phase and finds its complete input, and nothing is
built twice or in the wrong order.
