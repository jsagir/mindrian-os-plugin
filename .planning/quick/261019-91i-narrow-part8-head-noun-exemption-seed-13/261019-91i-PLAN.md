## The rule to build (rule R)

1. METHODOLOGY_HEAD_NOUNS goes back to the 14 nouns of commit 99bbbaa66. All old checks keep reading only this set. The old nouns then behave exactly as before 012224653.
2. A new frozen set TITLECASE_TOOL_HEAD_NOUNS holds `interview`, `check`, `whys`.
3. After the old checks fail for the candidate word at index j, walk right from j over directly adjacent TitleCase words (`adjacent` and `isTitle`, no function-word bridge). Stop at the first word whose `lc` is in TITLECASE_TOOL_HEAD_NOUNS and whose surface form is TitleCase. The candidate is exempt when that noun is at an index greater than j, or when the noun is at j and `words[j - 1]` exists, is adjacent, and is TitleCase. Otherwise the rule returns true (block).
4. Effect: the noun exempts only the TitleCase words directly before it, plus itself. It never exempts a word after it, a word across "of" or "the", or a word near a lowercase "check" or "interview".
