# Quick 261002-0n4: SEED-104 research grant family loop fix Summary

A Eureka plan now fetches after one approval, from a room with no grant and from a room holding a
whitespace-only standing grant. No room prose can become a composed query, an approved term or a
fetched string, and a stuck grant scope returns a typed error instead of another unanswerable card.

## What was wrong (root cause)

`STANDING_SCOPE` (Phase 363 D-04) hard-coded `whitespace-gap/v1`. Every approval minted a
whitespace-only standing grant, so a Eureka plan (concept-evidence/v1 queries) failed
`validateExecutedQuery` with `outside_family`, `run_quick` built another card, and approving it
again could never change the grant (`extendTerms` never touched families). Fixing the loop would
have un-blocked sends of room prose (a claim's "title" is its first sentence), so the egress gate
and the loop guard ship in the same change set.
