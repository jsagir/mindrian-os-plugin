Plan 240-04 builds the reachability so a non-transition turn can reach it. **R-06, the counter lives in the Layer 1 room-filesystem tier, written by `jtbd-state.cjs`, never by the Layer 2 module.** 240-RESEARCH.md's Architectural Responsibility Map carries an explicit tier-assignment warning: the D-01 invariant at `across-session-memory.cjs:33-36` forbids the Layer 2 module from writing Layer 1 state, and the counter is per-room within-session data anyway. 240-PATTERNS.md Correction 2 states the same conclusion from the other direction: `lib/hmi/jtbd-state.cjs` is a REQUIRED modify target the original dispatch brief omitted, because `setCurrent` is the only writer of `current` and the fields are structurally droppable nowhere else.

**R-07, `bumpTurnCount` is a NEW narrow writer, and it is the right shape rather than a second call to `setCurrent`.** A non-transition turn must not call `setCurrent`: that would rewrite `entered_at` (silently destroying the dwell signal), append one history row per turn, and blow through `HISTORY_MAX = 50`. 240-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3 names all three consequences. 240-RESEARCH.md:397 authorises the alternative in its own words: "`jtbd-state.cjs` must carry `turn_count` onto `newCurrent` (or expose a small bump helper)". This plan does BOTH, because the transition turn needs the field initialised and the non-transition turn needs it incremented. Canon Part 7 is satisfied by reusing `writeStateAtomic` and `readState` rather than adding a second I/O path.

**R-08, the counter starts at 1 on a transition and `NOISE_FLOOR_TURNS` stays 3.** The transition turn IS turn 1 on the new topic, so `setCurrent` writes `turn_count: 1` and two later same-topic turns reach 3. 240-RESEARCH.md Open Question Q2 recommends keeping 3 for continuity with both existing thresholds (`NOISE_FLOOR_TURNS` at `across-session-memory.cjs:76` and `VENTURE_NUDGE_THRESHOLD` at `venture-shape-nudge.cjs:59`), and warns against inventing a new number with no empirical baseline, since nobody has ever observed this promotion firing on real work. Read this before touching line 395.** There is a DOUBLE mismatch and 240-RESEARCH.md warns that fixing one leg leaves the gate red.
