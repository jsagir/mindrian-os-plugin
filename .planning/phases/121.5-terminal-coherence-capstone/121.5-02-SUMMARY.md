## ODD 4 resolution path chosen

**Chosen path: documentation-as-explicit-exception** (mirrors the existing no-emoji + statusline carve-out pattern).

**Alternative rejected: renumbering glyphs + renaming "JTBD" the word.**

**Rationale (4 points):**

1. **Phase 121.5 is consolidation, not expansion** (Canon Part 7). Renaming "JTBD" the word ripples through 24+ files, Brain context packets, telemetry event_types. Cost-benefit too high for the closing milestone.
2. **The collisions are real but CONTEXT-DISAMBIGUATING:** 🎯 in statusline Row 2 next to "JTBD" means "what job"; 🎯 in `/mos:jtbd` output means "broadcast"; 🎯 in EXPLORATION_LABELS means problem-definition. Each surface owns its position-anchored meaning. The three meanings never collide on the same surface at the same time.
3. **Documenting the overload + carve-out is what SKILL.md section 3 already does** (the no-emoji rule with the statusline exception). We follow the same precedent.
4. **Renumber / rename can ship as a v1.14.0 cleanup;** for v1.13.0 final we mark the seam, not move it. Tracked in `skills/ui-system/rules/glyph-disambiguation.md` section 4 (The v1.14.0 cleanup proposal).
