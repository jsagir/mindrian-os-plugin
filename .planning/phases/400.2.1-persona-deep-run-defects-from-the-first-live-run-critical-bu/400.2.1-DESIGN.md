## S4: relevance floor on door findings (INTERIM)
- Change: `wireAndRecord` checks each finding before the wire call. The handle words are the words of the approved string minus the hat qualifier and minus a closed list of function words (of, the, a, an, in, on, for, to, and, or, that, with, by, from, before, after), in order, each with a final s removed. The text words lose the same function words. A finding passes when its title, or its summary, holds two handle words next to each other (pairs are built separately for each text), or when title and summary hold every handle word. A one-word handle needs that word.
- A dropped finding is not wired. The run output gives `dropped_below_floor.<hat>`, and each string gives `dropped_below_floor` and `dropped` (up to 10 titles, after stripInjectionSpans, and urls). A string whose findings were all dropped is `fetched_not_wired:below_relevance_floor`; a yellow `none_found` after such a search has the basis `door_search_empty`. The evidence pack keeps its closed key list. commands/persona.md Step 5.6 prints the count.

Green drops 3 of the observed homonyms and keeps "Natural Law Theory" (law theory is a content pair). White, black and yellow lose many on-topic items. The floor is interim; a semantic reranker is the full fix and is not in this phase.
