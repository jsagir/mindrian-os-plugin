<objective>
Close the growth loop on every MCP surface: turn the pre-filled proposals that unattended halts already carry into a follow-up "Reject and never do this" gate, and land an entry only when the navigator approves it.

Purpose: D-10 (lands only with an approval trail), D-14 (every unattended halt and every Reject on a material-step card offers "Reject and never do this", pre-filled), D-15 (presented as a floor). The design reuses the house pattern research.cjs already uses for grants: a single-use material_step gate whose resumeFn performs the write after gate_answer mints the decision node. This keeps gate.cjs and every Theo-mirrored tool schema unchanged (the coordinator's parity constraint) instead of adding a proposed_constraint field to gate_render. The option is carried on a follow-up card, not as a fourth option, because chain.cjs maps the chosen option id straight to the verdict.
Output: lib/mcp/never-do-gate.cjs, chain.cjs and research.cjs changes, tests/test-365-never-do-gate.cjs.
</objective>
