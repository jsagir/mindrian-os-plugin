Subject: One decision needed on the verification ladder

**Summary**

We built what you asked for: when a claim is approved, the room now says in plain words what the claim was checked against, and a claim checked only by asking a model cannot be filed as confirmed. A room can also keep a "never do this" list that unattended work respects.

The part that depends on you is the ladder itself. We need you to ratify or amend the six draft rungs, from "unchecked" up to "a person with standing who disagreed", because the next piece of work writes those rungs into the record and we will not build it on a ladder you have not approved.

**The one decision**

Do you ratify the six draft rungs as written, or do you want to amend them?

The questions behind that decision are listed in the appendix. They are optional: a one-line answer on the rungs is enough to unblock us, and we can take the rest in whatever order suits you.

**Appendix (optional): the questions behind the decision**

1. The six draft rungs. Please ratify or amend each:
   - 0 Unchecked: filed and not tested.
   - 1 Navigator recall or intuition.
   - 2 Model-internal: asked a model, got agreement.
   - 3 Secondary document: a summary, an aggregator, an abstract, a plain-language version.
   - 4 Primary source, located: the specific part that decides the claim.
   - 5 A person with standing who disagreed, or who could have and did not.

2. What each rung must show. Please ratify or amend each:
   - From rung 3 up, the claim links to a source with a web address and a retrieval date.
   - From rung 4 up, that source also names the deciding part (a page, a section, a DOI or a clause).
   - Rung 5 needs a link to a person who is recorded as having standing and as having disagreed, or as having had the chance and not taken it.

3. The earlier records. Earlier checking records use five provisional names: own intuition, model counter-argument, database or document, primary source, dissenting person. Should they map onto the ratified ladder (and how), or should those checks be recorded again?

4. A person with standing. For rung 5 the room needs a record of a person, a new kind of thing in the room. What makes someone a person with standing for a given claim? Who decides, and what must be written down about them?

5. The default. Today a room asks, by default, that an approved claim be checked against at least a source document before it counts as confirmed. Is that the right default? A room can raise or lower it.

6. The words. The provisional wording shows a claim as, for example, "checked against a source document outside the conversation" or "recorded as checked only by asking a model". Does that read as legible without punishing? If a low rung reads like a failure it is the wrong wording; in your own framing, most claims sitting at rungs 1 and 2 means the fix is working.

**What waits on your answer**

The rung worked out from the room's own links, so a filer can never declare it; the record of a person; a split of the "no support" scan by kind of silence; and carrying the earlier records over. Your experiment of a disciplined person with a chat window and a notebook against the room also waits on the worked-out rung.
