Subject: Six rungs for you to ratify or amend

Your build spec asked for a rung a claim earns, not one the person filing it declares. Most of that is now built, and the rest is waiting on you. Here is what shipped while we waited, and then the questions only you can answer.

**What is working now (all of it uses provisional words)**

- When someone approves a claim, the card now says in plain words what the claim was checked against, before the click. If the room asks for a source document and the claim was only checked by asking a model, approving files it as "needs evidence", not "confirmed". There is no "confirm anyway" button.
- Each room can write down a "never do this" list. Unattended steps (a chain of commands, or research that starts on its own) stop and ask when they would touch something on that list. The list only grows when the navigator approves an entry, and it says plainly that it catches only what has been named.
- Asked later, the room says in words what each claim was checked against, and one request shows the whole room as counts in words, each with what would move it. No scores, no percentages.

**What we need from you**

1. The six draft rungs. Please ratify or amend each:
   - 0 Unchecked: filed and not tested.
   - 1 Navigator recall or intuition.
   - 2 Model-internal: asked a model, got agreement.
   - 3 Secondary document: a summary, an aggregator, an abstract, a plain-language version.
   - 4 Primary source, located: the specific part that decides the claim.
   - 5 A person with standing who disagreed, or who could have and did not.

2. The three structural predicates. Please ratify or amend each:
   - From rung 3 up, the claim has a link to a source that carries a web address and a retrieval date.
   - From rung 4 up, that source also names the deciding part (a page, a section, a DOI or a clause).
   - Rung 5 needs a link to a person who is recorded as having standing and as having disagreed, or as having had the chance and not taken it.

3. The old records. Earlier checking records use five provisional rung names: own intuition, model counter-argument, database or document, primary source, dissenting person. Should they map onto the ratified ladder (and how), or should those checks be recorded again?

4. A person with standing. For rung 5 the room needs a record of a person, which is a new kind of thing in the room. What makes someone "a person with standing" for a given claim? Who decides, and what must be written down about them?

5. The default floor. Today every room asks, by default, that an approved claim be checked against at least a source document before it counts as confirmed. Is a source document the right default floor? A room can raise or lower it in one line of its own settings.

6. The words. The provisional wording shows each claim as, for example, "checked against a source document outside the conversation" or "recorded as checked only by asking a model". Does this read as legible without punishing? If a low rung reads like a failure, it is the wrong wording; in your own framing, most claims sitting at rungs 1 and 2 is the fix working.

**What waits on your answer**

The rung that is worked out from the room's own links (so a filer can never declare it), the person record, a split of the "no support" scan by kind of silence, and the migration of the old records. Your discipline-control experiment (a person with a chat window and a notebook against the room) also waits on the worked-out rung, since that is what lets the room answer "which of these claims was never really checked".
