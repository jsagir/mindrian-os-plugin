The seven operations, named exactly as the graph names its ProcessSteps (363-SCIENTIFIC-ROADMAPPING-RESEARCH-BUILDER.md): Tension Qualification, Goal Quantification, Rung Placement and Type Selection, Forum Construction, Path Enumeration, Constraint Interrogation, Catalytic Ranking; the loop is 7 -> 1.

Roadmap types by rung (the article's step 3): UnDefined -> Landscape or Vision (idea kind: reframings); IllDefined -> Manifesto or Technical Roadmap (programs); WellDefined -> Pipeline or Opportunity (optimizations); Wicked -> Landscape (a bounded systems map; "unresolved" is a valid outcome, per 363-RESEARCH-pws.md's Wicked row) [disclosed default]. Unknown rung -> no roadmap type check, a warning to place the rung.

Engine choice: engine 'scientific-roadmapping' when the plan's scientific flag is true (363-10 detectScientific) or the template is scientific-roadmapping; otherwise 'constraint-layer': the same operations and the same two columns, with the forum's physics grounder read as "the one who can tell what is truly fixed". Depth 'lite' is allowed only for quick plans (tension, falsifier and at least one limiter required; forum, paths and ranking optional); deep plans require 'full'.
