Family wording (disclosed defaults, tuned against replay fixtures in 363-20; every template renders quotes and operators itself, never the model):
- whitespace-gap/v1 (the only family a standing grant may cover in 363, D-04): ws.exact `"{term}"` role primary; ws.synonym_cover `"{term}" OR "{syn1}" [OR "{syn2}" OR "{syn3}"]` role falsifier_covered_elsewhere (1 to 3 synonyms); ws.prior_attempts `"{term}" AND (review OR survey OR "systematic review")` role falsifier_tried_before; ws.absence_reason `"{term}" AND (limitation OR barrier OR infeasible)` role context.
- concept-evidence/v1: ce.exact `"{term}"` primary; ce.counter `"{term}" AND (limitation OR failure OR "no effect")` falsifier; ce.prior_success `"{term}" AND (success OR adoption OR "case study")` prior; ce.alternative `"{term}" AND (alternative OR substitute)` alternative; ce.pair `"{term}" AND "{term2}"` pair.
- causal-link/v1: cl.link `"{cause}" AND "{effect}"` primary; cl.break `"{cause}" AND "{effect}" AND (confound OR "no association" OR "not associated")` falsifier.
- constraint-interrogation/v1 (D-18 step 6): ci.derivation `"{limiter}" AND ("fundamental limit" OR "theoretical limit" OR bound)` derivation; ci.retest `"{limiter}" AND (overcome OR circumvent OR "new approach")` retest; ci.scurve `"{limiter}" AND (saturation OR plateau OR "diminishing returns")` scurve; ci.prior_attack `"{limiter}" AND (review OR survey)` prior.
- diffusion/v1 (D-19): df.adoption `"{technology}" AND (adoption OR diffusion)` first_adopters; df.capacity `"{technology}" AND ("absorptive capacity" OR "technology transfer")` absorptive_capacity; df.dualuse `"{technology}" AND ("dual-use" OR "dual use" OR defense)` civil_defense_crossing; df.timing `"{technology}" AND ("S-curve" OR "adoption rate" OR "technology readiness")` timing.

Slot rules: a term is 2 to 80 characters after trimming, contains no double quote, newline, parenthesis, backslash, or standalone AND, OR or NOT token in any case, and is audited on its own; synonyms are 1 to 3 distinct terms under the same rules; the composed string is at most 200 characters (require `MAX_QUERY_CHARS` from `lib/core/dominant-design/lane-queries.cjs`, reuse not copy) and single-line.
