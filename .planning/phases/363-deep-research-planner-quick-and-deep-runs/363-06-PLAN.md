Template content (data, written in our own words from the shipped references; dimension ids are stable API):
- map-unknowns (framework "Knowns and Unknowns Matrix Framework", door /mos:map-unknowns), from references/methodology/map-unknowns.md "The Matrix": mu:known_known (researchable 'verify': validate the fact; falsifier: evidence contradicting it), mu:blind_spot (known to others, unknown to you: the purest D-00 source; researchable), mu:hidden_known (you know it but have not said it: not researchable externally, reason "tacit knowledge the team must surface"), mu:unknown_unknown (not researchable, reason "unknowable by definition"; an optional mece_gap leaf may ask what adjacent evidence would reveal it). - root-cause (framework "Root Cause Analysis", door /mos:root-cause), from references/methodology/root-cause.md: rc:why_link (each "why" answered by an assumption; causal-link family; falsifier: the link breaks or a confound explains it) and the Fishbone 6M dims rc:6m_man, rc:6m_machine, rc:6m_method, rc:6m_material, rc:6m_measurement, rc:6m_nature (coverage required only when the question set declares multi_cause true; otherwise marked not researchable with reason "single causal chain").
- think-hats (framework "Six Thinking Hats", door /mos:think-hats), from references/methodology/think-hats.md: hat:white (missing facts), hat:black (counterevidence), hat:yellow (prior successes), hat:green (alternatives; feeds perspective paths), hat:red (not researchable, reason "feelings, not literature"), hat:blue (the governing question itself; not a lane, reason "process hat frames the pyramid top").
- whitespace (framework: read the exact `frameworks:` value of commands/whitespace.md and use it verbatim, door /mos:whitespace): ws:gap_claim, ws:covered_elsewhere (falsifier: covered under another term), ws:irrelevant (not researchable, reason "navigator judgment"), ws:extraction_failure (corpus room: a local check, no egress). Opportunity rule: ws:gap_claim with verdict gap-confirmed -> literature_gap.
- diffusion (framework "Adoption-Capacity Theory", door /mos:diffusion, D-19): df:first_adopters (who adopts first), df:absorptive_capacity, df:civil_defense_crossing (the dual-use crossing), df:timing (adoption timing and S-curve position; its scurve rows feed the Scientific Roadmapping step 6 reading). Opportunity rule: df:timing with an S-curve reading of near_ceiling on the incumbent and headroom on the challenger -> trend_break.
- scientific-roadmapping (framework "Scientific Roadmapping", doors ['/mos:research'] in 363, reusable by SEED-098, D-18): sr:tension, sr:goal, sr:rung, sr:forum_insider, sr:forum_entrant, sr:forum_grounder, sr:paths, sr:paths_10x, sr:limiters, sr:ranking; lenses ci.derivation, ci.retest, ci.scurve, ci.prior_attack (constraint-interrogation family, 363-08). Opportunity rule: an assumed limiter with unlock-chain length at least 1 -> constraint_attack.
Every template declares, per dimension, `default_lens`, `default_family` (whitespace-gap/v1, concept-evidence/v1, causal-link/v1, constraint-interrogation/v1 or diffusion/v1) and `falsifier_template` (a template id of that family, or null for a local or not-researchable dimension), plus `perspective_step` (tension, goal, rung, forum, paths, limiters, ranking) saying where the dimension lands in the D-18 perspective.
