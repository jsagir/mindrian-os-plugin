<objective>
Build the quick research run end to end in code: check the grant, fetch cache-first, validate rows, compute the verdict, return an evidence card, offer escalation once.

Purpose: D-03 (quick is one pass with an evidence card and a code-computed verdict; the room may start it under a standing grant; a thin or contested card offers "run deep on this?" and seeds the deep plan), D-04 (grant check and audit per query), D-07 (opportunity candidates on the card), D-16 (failure never becomes a gap). Rows can come from the host model or, with no model present, from deterministicTermRows, so the same run works in the ambient child.
Output: `verdict.cjs`, `quick.cjs`, floor-ledger rows for the budgets and floor, `tests/test-363-run-quick.cjs`.
</objective>
