# Phase 187.1 - Statusline battery-memory + voice-switch recalibration

## Why this phase exists

Dogfood session 2026-06-29 (navigator Jonathan Sagir). Phase 187 shipped the four-tier cockpit
(beta.9) with a `Ctx <n>%` risk chip. Two navigator findings drove this sub-phase:

1. **`Ctx <n>%` is meaningless AND inverted for a non-technical navigator.** A person reads a
   percentage like a battery: "8%" looks nearly empty and triggers panic, when 8% USED means 92%
   FREE -- the bar said danger while the truth was safe. The fix reframes the risk tier as a battery
   counting REMAINING conversational memory, in plain language ("memory left" / "memory almost full -
   save your work now"). High = good, matching the phone/laptop battery mental model.

2. **The Voice Signature (Part 12) showed a move glyph but never named WHO.** The navigator must see
   at a glance whether they are hearing Larry (thinking partner) or the native host (raw tool). The
   identity tier now carries a person/robot glyph: `👤 Larry` vs `🤖 <agent>`. Brain backing + the
   pedagogical-move voice square render for Larry only.

A third finding became a SPEC (not built this phase): when the voice SWITCHES (larry<->claude, both
directions), a tripwire should invoke an F.7 recalibration dial -- the Part 12 Modality Remote
(UP/DOWN ask-tell, LEFT/RIGHT challenge-converge) + a WHO pick + checkbox extras + free-text explain.
The statusline detects + displays the switch (passive); the F.7 dial fires in the conversation.

## What shipped (implementation, green)

- `lib/statusline/cockpit-renderer.cjs`: `memoryChip()` (battery, remaining); `PERSON`/`ROBOT`/
  `BATTERY`/`BATTERY_LOW` glyphs; `CLIFF_MSG` -> plain "memory almost full - save your work now";
  `_render` reworked: WHO segment (`👤 Larry` / `🤖 <agent>`), Brain + voice glyph gated to Larry,
  battery in the risk tier, low-battery + plain message as the cliff hero.
- `lib/statusline/cockpit-signals.cjs`: `who` + `agent_label` threaded into the cockpit state
  (default `larry` -- the conversational surface IS Larry, Part 10; `claude` only on explicit
  non-Larry agent).
- `scripts/context-monitor`: extracts `data.agent.name`, derives `who`/`agent_label` into rowState
  (default larry; `/larry/i` stays larry), passes through `signalOpts`.
- `tests/test-statusline-cockpit-187.cjs`: assertions updated to the battery + WHO + plain-cliff
  contract. 117/117 green.
- `docs/STATUSLINE-CONTRACT.md`: PROPOSED v2 section -- the voice-switch tripwire + F.7 recalibration
  dial (spec only).
