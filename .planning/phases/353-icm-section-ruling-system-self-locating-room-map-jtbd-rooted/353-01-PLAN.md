<objective>
Give every folder of an ICM room self-knowledge it can answer from itself, with one rebuildable home for the fact and one derived, fingerprinted view per folder.

Purpose: self-location is the addressing layer the section ruling system in plan 02 is written on top of. Without it, a ruling document has no reliable answer to "which section am I", and the filing gate in plan 02 has nothing to resolve a section against (R2, D-353-2).
Output: `lib/core/room-map.cjs`, the `icm_self` block writer wired into the scaffolder and into sub-room birth as side effect six, the `room-map` doctor module, the birth-time job declaration card and its `job_id` write on the child ROOM.md, the additive `legE` self-location read on `getRoomContext`, the phase aggregator, the fixture room tree, and a report-mode-only fleet evidence file.
</objective>
