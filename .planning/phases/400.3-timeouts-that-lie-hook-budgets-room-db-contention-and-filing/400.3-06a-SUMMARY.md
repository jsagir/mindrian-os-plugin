## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] EPIPE race in the holder stop of the tests**
- Found during: Task 1 RED run.
- Issue: a timed holder (holdMs) can exit while the parent is inside a synchronous busy wait. A later `child.send('release')` then emitted an unhandled EPIPE error and crashed the test process.
- Fix: each forked holder gets `child.on('error', ...)`, and 'release' is sent with a callback that kills the child on error. Applied in the new test file and in startDbLockHolder and stopDbLockHolder of tests/test-400-3-room-root-detection.cjs. No arm changed.
