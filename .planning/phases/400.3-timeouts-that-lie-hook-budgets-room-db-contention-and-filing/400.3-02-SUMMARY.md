## Open conflicts (plan arm against code at BASE; not resolved here)

1. **C10 wait arm.** The plan says the index under a held SQLite write transaction reports room_db_busy after 600 ms or more. Measured: room_db_busy after 2-3 ms. Cause (PROVEN with a debug script): lazygraph-ops.cjs indexArtifact (BASE code, not changed) opens a deferred BEGIN and reads before its first INSERT (insertNode, node-insert.cjs:251). In WAL mode, SQLite returns SQLITE_BUSY at once on that read-to-write upgrade and does not call the busy handler. So the openGraph busyMs wait (D-11) is not used on this path. The reason word is right, and the at-most bound holds (D-11 says "at most about 750 ms"). Only the lower bound fails. Possible fix (not done; design choice): take the write lock at the start (BEGIN IMMEDIATE) in the index path. P6 of plan 01 passes because initSchema on a bare room writes first, so the busy handler is used there.
2. **H5.** The plan says a Write under a held write.lock gives "index failed (index only)" and a spawn wall time under 3000 ms. Measured: 10,401-11,744 ms, and the advisory says "indexed". Cause (PROVEN): post-write runs `minto-debouncer.cjs enqueue` in the foreground before the node call. Under a held lock it retries 12 times and gives up after about 11-14 s. Directly measured: 13,416 ms now and 14,551 ms with the BASE debouncer. By then the 10 s holder has released the lock, so the index succeeds. The same arm took 10,783 ms on the unchanged post-write. This is a PRE-EXISTING hook budget breach under a held lock, and it is not caused by this plan. Plan 01 kept the debouncer constants on purpose (T-400.3-07). Possible fix (not done; design choice): a bounded wait for the foreground enqueue, or background it (plan 400.3-04 or 400.3-08 territory).
