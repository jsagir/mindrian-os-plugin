Build the greenfield four-arrow register HUD and its synchronous CONTROL path.

The navigator can grab the dial/posture wheel with the keyboard at the same pre-compose
latency as a typed escape phrase. Up/Down arrows mutate investment_level; Left/Right arrows
mutate posture over the frozen 3 postures. The HUD is a NEW pure module
(lib/hmi/dial-selector.cjs); it is wired into the selector-dispatcher as a new shape
branch mirroring the F.7 branch (selector-dispatcher.cjs:732). The two axes already exist
(investment_level + POSTURE_IDS) -- this plan does NOT mint a reach or a posture; it lets
the human MOVE the existing two axes.

share -- "just tell me" is prompt-only. So BCH-16's CONTROL path is greenfield: it must
ESTABLISH the pre-compose register_override scratch that the compose step reads at turn
top. The AUDIT write (manual_override @1.0, post-pipeline) is explicitly Wave 2 and is NOT
in this plan.

Purpose: give the human a same-turn, same-latency hand on the dial that the engine reads
BEFORE it composes (BCH-16 CONTROL is synchronous and pre-compose).
Output: lib/hmi/dial-selector.cjs, a dial shape branch in the dispatcher, and green
test-bch-10-register-hud + test-bch-16-keypress-latency suites.
</objective>
