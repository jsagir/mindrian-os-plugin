Wave 2 (depends_on 177-01). They are PURE deterministic gates -- not semantic cue seams
(the model-cue seams BCH-S3/S6/S4b are Wave 4, behind BCH-CAL).

in framing mode and saturation is caught by math, not vibes.

test-bch-s5-turn-stage-eligibility + test-bch-s4a-saturation suites.
