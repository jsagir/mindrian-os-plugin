The truth machine stops being a design and becomes a running system.
Output: the accept-branch wiring in selector-decisions.cjs, the confirmedFacts contract fix in room-home.cjs, the instrumented acceptance test, the run-all-129.5.sh aggregator, and Feynman-runner registration.
