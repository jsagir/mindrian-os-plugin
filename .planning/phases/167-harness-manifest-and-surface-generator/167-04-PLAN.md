<objective>
WAVE 4 GENERATOR (HARN-03, D-167-05): ship /mos:new-surface -- the harness-as-code onboarding front door
for SURFACES. Given a new command / agent / skill, it emits the correct surface .md carrying the 11-key
connector frontmatter (the docs/CONNECTOR-CONTRACT.md contract: connects_to_spine, sensor_triggers,
reach_id, sub_mode, framework, posture, hierarchy_rank, filing, plan_gated, web_scope, surface) so wiring
is NEVER hand-written, then regenerates the connector-registry (the surface's real home) and the
harness-manifest (whose wiring digest then reflects the new surface). A --check gate (mirroring Wave 1 /
D-167-01 + D-167-03) proves the emitted surface is WELL-FORMED, REGISTERED in connector-registry.json, and
that the harness-manifest --check is clean.

TRANSITIVE LANDING, NOT A PER-SURFACE MANIFEST ROW (HIGH-1 reconciliation): the harness-manifest is a
3-map digest (Wave 1) and carries NO per-surface rows. A new surface therefore does NOT land as a manifest
entry. It lands TRANSITIVELY: (1) build-new-surface.cjs emits the surface .md with the 11 connector keys;
(2) it shells out to regenerate scripts/build-connector-registry.cjs -> data/connector-registry.json, which
is the surface's REAL home (the per-surface registry picks up the new surface from its frontmatter);
(3) it shells out to regenerate scripts/build-harness-manifest.cjs -> data/harness-manifest.json, whose
wiring entry digest + source_count then change to reflect the larger connector-registry. The Wave-4 --check
asserts (a) the surface .md exists with all 11 connector keys validated against connector-registry (its real
home), and (b) build-harness-manifest.cjs --check is clean (the wiring digest reflects the new surface). It
does NOT assert a per-surface manifest entry, because none exists -- D-166-03 stays inviolate.

D-167-05 (reuse the new-project SCAFFOLD-BACKEND, NOT ignite): /mos:ignite is the conversational FRONT DOOR
(the B1/B2/B3 birth gates, the Hooked first-cycle, the birthRoom transaction) -- it ORCHESTRATES.
/mos:new-project is the SCAFFOLD BACKEND it delegates to (deterministic file emission + frontmatter) -- it
EMITS files. HARN-03 needs the MECHANICS (new-project's half), NOT ignite's gate orchestration. So
new-surface reuses the new-project scaffold-backend PATTERN + the 11-key connector frontmatter contract
(docs/CONNECTOR-CONTRACT.md, build-connector-registry.cjs:89-101). IMPORTANT: even this is PATTERN reuse,
NOT literal extension -- new-project scaffolds a ROOM (folders / sections / ROOM.md), /mos:new-surface
scaffolds a SURFACE (a command / agent / skill .md + its registry/manifest regeneration), a different
artifact. new-surface borrows new-project's file-emission + frontmatter-contract + --check idiom, adapted
to surfaces. /mos:ignite is OUT of scope and untouched.

Surface != room (167 hard rules): pattern reuse, not literal extension; ignite OUT of scope; bulk backfill
of the 96 commands' wiring OUT of scope (167-SPEC out-of-scope -- the generator makes per-surface wiring
cheap; backfill is its own follow-on). The emitted reach_id is one of the frozen 6 and the posture one of
the frozen 3 (sensor-types.REACH_IDS / POSTURE_IDS); the generator mints NO new reach_id (a new tool
identity rides sub_mode, never a 7th reach).

SHELL-OUT, NOT require()+call internals (LOW-2 ruling): after emitting the surface .md, build-new-surface.cjs
MUST regenerate the two downstream registries via child_process.execFileSync('node', [...]) on fresh
processes -- first execFileSync('node', ['scripts/build-connector-registry.cjs']), then
execFileSync('node', ['scripts/build-harness-manifest.cjs']) -- NOT by require()-ing those generators and
calling their internals. A fresh process gives the identical, byte-stable serialize path each generator
ships with and avoids any module-cache bleed (a required-and-mutated generator could serialize against a
stale in-process cache). This is why MEDIUM-2 is closed by a real regeneration step, not a hand-edit.

Reuse before build (Canon Part 7): the generator + --check idiom is a VERBATIM mirror of the shipped
generators (build-connector-registry.cjs main:594-662, the 11-key CONNECTOR_KEYS:89-101; the Wave-1
build-harness-manifest.cjs --check). The connector frontmatter shape is the SHIPPED new-project /
ignite block (new-project.md:19-30). The deterministic-emit pattern is new-project's scaffold backend. A
rewrite is rejected; the contract, the keys, and the --check idiom ship and are reused in shape. The
genuine net-new is the surface-emitter + the /mos:new-surface command surface.

Output: commands/new-surface.md, scripts/build-new-surface.cjs (emit + regenerate + --check), the
regenerated data/connector-registry.json, the generator test, and its registration in run-all-167.sh. NO
em-dashes anywhere (CLAUDE.md HARD RULE).
