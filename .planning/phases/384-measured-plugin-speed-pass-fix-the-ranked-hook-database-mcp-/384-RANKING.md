## Verdict rule

This rule is fixed by plan 384-09 and is applied the same way to every row. Two clauses (V1 for more than two sets, and V6 for a cost that has one set only) fill cases the plan text left open. The author added them after reading the measurement files, and states that here.

V1 Spread is always a percent: |m1 - m2| / mean x 100 of two sets of the same tree. For more than two sets it is the largest |mi - mj| divided by the mean of all set medians, times 100.
V2 Noise turns a spread into the unit of a number: noise = spread percent / 100 x the median of that number.
V3 A DIRECT cost is one measured number and has the noise of that measure. A DERIVED cost is a product or a difference of two or more measures. Its noise is the sum of the noises of its parts, each in the unit of the cost. For a product A x B the noise is B x noise(A) + A x noise(B), and its spread percent is the sum of the spread percents of the parts. For a difference the noise is the sum of the noises of the two parts.
V4 The frequency of an event per session is a count with no spread. A cost per session is the cost per occurrence times the frequency and keeps the noise of the cost, scaled by the frequency.
V5 The median cell is the mean of the two set medians (or the median of the single run that exists). The p95 cell is the larger of the two set p95 values. p95 is the sorted sample at index floor(0.95 x (n - 1)).
V6 A cost that has one set only takes its spread from the two-set spread of the nearest measure of the same event on the fixture room. The row says so. A copy row of that kind carries the label `spread from fixture`. That label is the noise basis only for the copy row of the same target.
V7 Mechanism observed means that the counted number or call pattern in the measurement is at least 80 percent of the PRD claim, or that the described read, open, spawn or watch exists.
V8 CONFIRMED: the mechanism was observed and the cost is larger than 2 times its noise. REFUTED: the mechanism was not observed, or the cost is not larger than 2 times its noise. NOT MEASURABLE: no tool in this phase could observe it; the gap is named. Each row has exactly one verdict.
V9 Absolute floor: a confirmed cost must also be larger than the smallest saving per event that matters to a person. The owner has not set it (question Q5b). Every row shows `meets floor` as `pending Q5b`, and CONFIRMED stays provisional until Q5b is answered.
V10 Share of the felt path = cost per session divided by the felt wall per session (section Felt path). A cost that is a serial sum of parallel work is shown as an upper bound. A background cost has no share of the felt path.
V11 Cost definition of each finding. S1 = node starts counted by the census times the floor:node median (DERIVED), shown next to the bash -x trace shares, which are a diagnostic and not the cost. S2 = summed CPU milliseconds of the blocking hooks of the event (DERIVED: a sum of per-hook medians; its noise is the sum of the noises of the hooks). S3 = median cold openRoomDb time times the opens counted per SessionStart (DERIVED). S4 = background time after return and survivor CPU per SubagentStop at 1, 5 and 20 files (DIRECT, lower bound). S5 = drain hook median minus the floor median, paired (DERIVED); for the auto-explore drain, the read count against the number of finding files, and the time at 100 files minus 0 files (DERIVED). S6 = per-call read time at 10,000 rows minus at 100 rows (DERIVED), times the writes per session for the per-session cost. S7 = the initialize reply per MCP start (DIRECT), or NOT MEASURED if an npm child started. S8 = ping latency during stop_gate_check minus the idle ping (DERIVED). S9 = readTurnText cold median on 4 MiB times the transcript reads per Stop counted (DERIVED). S10 = the process with the watcher minus the bare node control, for idle RSS, idle CPU in the window and watch count, at 1 room and at 58 rooms (DERIVED); its unit is MB, CPU milliseconds and watches, not felt time.
V12 Order of The five: first the CONFIRMED findings with blocking cost, by cost per session, largest first; then the CONFIRMED background findings, each by its own unit, in the order of its share of the measured background total. The first five are proposed. If fewer than five are CONFIRMED, the list is short and says why.

Units to read with care. S2 is a CPU sum over hooks that run side by side, so it is larger than the wait. S1 is a serial sum of start-up floors of processes that run side by side, so it is an upper bound on the wait. S8 and S4 and S10 are in their own units.
