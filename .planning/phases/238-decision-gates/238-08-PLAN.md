<objective>
Stop the card-fire backstop from punishing ordinary prose, without deleting the arm that exists for when the side channel itself fails, and turn the corpus test from 238-07 green.

Purpose (GATE-04, SC3): `238-RESEARCH.md` measured that pure regex provably cannot separate a footnote reference list from a bracket-box gate, because `[1] Simon 1962.` newline `[2] Rittel and Webber 1973.` and `[1] Build the plan` newline `[2] File the evidence` are the SAME string shape. Both candidate replacement patterns still fire on the footnote list, and line-anchoring additionally loses the same-line bracket-box true positive. This repo already paid for the alternative approach once: the nearby-cue co-requirement was retired at roughly an 86 percent false-positive rate.

The remedy is therefore architectural, not a better regex. Add a structural signal a citation cannot have: a corroborating side-channel reach record for this session. A citation list in prose has no gate mint behind it.

Purpose (the honest cost, ruled on and recorded): this demotes the backstop from an independent detector to a confirmer wherever the side channel is working. The navigator ruling accepts that trade AND requires the last-resort arm to survive, so the gating is conditional on side-channel HEALTH, not unconditional. See D-15.

Output: one new health export, the gated intercept decision, the corpus test green, both mutation legs demonstrated, and the open RCA honestly re-scoped.
</objective>

<phase_decisions>
- **D-15 (the GATE-04 remedy, ruled on):** Gate the backstop's INTERCEPT DECISION on side-channel corroboration WHERE AVAILABLE, and preserve the backstop's independent authority where the side channel itself has failed. Concretely, three states:
  1. Side channel healthy AND a fresh reach record exists for this session: backstop may intercept, exactly as today.
  2. Side channel healthy AND no reach record: backstop does NOT intercept. This is the state that kills the citation and footnote false positives.
  3. Side channel unavailable (module require failure, unreadable or unparseable side file): backstop retains full independent authority, exactly as today. This is the "detector of last resort" the backstop's own doctrine header claims, preserved rather than deleted.

  **The accepted residual, stated plainly and not hidden:** a genuine unrendered bracket-box gate from an OFF-REGISTRY surface, with a healthy side channel and no reach record, no longer intercepts. That is a real reduction in coverage. It is accepted because the PRIMARY arm handles 37 of 38 real fires in the live log, the backstop arm has 0 of 38, and the alternative is a classifier that fires on ordinary technical prose in a dev repo. This residual is recorded in the source comment block, in the corpus test's state-4 comment, and in the SUMMARY. It is NOT quietly omitted.

  Grounding recorded honestly: `langtalks-graph-expert` was consulted on guardrail and classifier false-positive patterns for structured-output versus prose detection and returned "not in the corpus", which is a valid outcome for that source and is not papered over. The `claude-code-guide` agent confirmed against real Claude Code documentation that there is NO documented way for a Stop hook to distinguish "an AskUserQuestion tool_use actually fired this turn" from "the assistant's text merely looks like a rendered card". That undocumented gap is precisely WHY tracking real mint events out of band (the side channel) is the architecturally correct discriminator rather than a smarter regex.
- **D-06 (both regexes stay byte-identical):** `ASCII_BOX_GLYPH_RE` is the retry-key anchor pinned verbatim by `tests/test-209-backstop-tuning.cjs`, and `ASCII_BOX_UNCONDITIONAL_RE` also stays byte-identical because the fix is at the decision layer. Re-keying the retry store is not in scope and would orphan live entries.
- **D-16 (conservative default on an unknown state):** a turn object that carries NEITHER new field (the direct-field unit-test envelope shape) must behave exactly as today. The gating branch fires only when health is explicitly the healthy value. This keeps every existing predicate assertion byte-stable and makes the fix additive rather than a rewrite.
- **D-17 (the open RCA is re-scoped, not closed):** the open stale-F1-reach RCA is a PRIMARY-arm defect. This phase's GATE-04 work is on the BACKSTOP arm and does not close it. It gets a dated re-scoping note naming what Phase 238 does and does not address, rather than being moved to `resolved/` on a false claim.
</phase_decisions>
