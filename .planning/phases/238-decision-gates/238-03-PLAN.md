<objective>
Re-point `gate_render` and `gate_answer` onto the shared ledger built in 238-02, and make `gate_answer` validate the submitted `chosen` against the card it actually minted before anything is persisted.

Purpose (GATE-01 G-1, gate side): `238-RESEARCH.md` reproduced live that `lib/mcp/tools/gate.cjs`'s `_liveGates` Map is one of two disconnected ledgers. Deleting the local Map and requiring `lib/mcp/gate-ledger.cjs` is the gate-side half of joining them; 238-04 does the chain-side half.

Purpose (GATE-01 G-2, gate side): reproduced live, `normalizeGateAnswer` copies `chosen` verbatim, and `gate.cjs` then opens the room DB and writes a `memory_event` carrying that `chosen` and returns `ratified: true`. The minted card is in hand at that exact line, because the consumed ledger entry carries it, and it is unused. The zod schema checks shape only. This is the canonical ASVS V5 value-domain gap.

Purpose (GATE-03 half A, gate side): `_mintLiveGate` records no session id at all, even though the handler computed one two lines earlier. Minting through `mintGate` with the resolved session id closes that.

Output: `gate.cjs` on the shared ledger with the allow-list reject in place, an honest tool description, and a test that proves no row is written on reject.
</objective>
