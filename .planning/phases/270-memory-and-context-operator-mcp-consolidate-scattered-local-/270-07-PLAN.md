<objective>
Build `discoverIcmForest(opts)`: the forest ROOT that composes the two walkers that already ship.

RESEARCH.md 3.1.1 tested the grounding claim that "no recursive whole-hierarchy walker exists" and
found it **literally true and materially misleading**. The name-grep returns zero hits, but the
CAPABILITY exists twice, plus a third detection mechanism:

- **Walker A, filesystem, marker-driven, depth-capped.** `walkFractalMemory`
  (`lib/core/memory/reconcile-memory-runner.cjs:174-195`) and `discoverMemoryFiles` (`:209-222`).
  Scans for the 7 classified memory basenames (`BASENAME_TO_KIND` at `:73-87`) and recurses via
  `sectionRegistry.discoverSections`. Its own header at `:144-145` states the discipline verbatim:
  "NO hand-rolled walker - Part 7 reuse". Capped at `DEPTH_CAP = 3` (`lib/core/coverage-rollup.cjs:41`).
- **Walker B, graph-native, arbitrary depth, cross-room.** `rollupSubRooms(parentRoomDir)`
  (`lib/core/graph-derivation.cjs`, header `:33-40`): recursive, transitive, cross-room read-only
  `ATTACH`, cycle-guarded by a visited-set of resolved db paths.
- **Mechanism C, detection of unregistered folders.** `detectUnsentineledArtifactFolder(roomDir)`
  (`lib/core/graph-self-heal.cjs:7-13`).

**The genuine gap is narrow and precisely nameable.** `room_list` (`lib/mcp/tools/room.cjs`,
`listRooms()` at `:41`) does a single-level `readdirSync` of `$MINDRIAN_ROOMS_HOME`. Walker A starts
at one already-resolved `roomDir`. Walker B starts at one already-resolved `parentRoomDir`. Nothing
composes "enumerate top-level rooms, then descend each one." **That composition is the only new
code.** Everything else is delegation, and `tests/test-270-no-second-walker.cjs` enforces it.

**One thing this plan must NOT do.** `healRoom`'s step (0), verbatim at `lib/core/graph-self-heal.cjs:21-23`:
"approvedBy gate. Without it, REFUSE with `{ok:false, reason:'no_approval'}` and write NOTHING (Part
3 Decision Gate; the room-birth.cjs:316 gate)." The registration step is CONSTITUTIONALLY REQUIRED.
Discovery may surface a folder as "found, unregistered". It may never promote one. Pitfall P4.

Purpose: make the ICM forest legible at runtime past the frozen baseline, without minting a walker,
a depth constant, or a promotion path.
Output: one new `lib/core` module, greening three of the four Wave-0 discovery pins.
