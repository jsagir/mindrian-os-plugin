Both the scanner's `desc` strings and the test's pattern-3 fixture are assembled from fragments so the source files themselves do not carry the contiguous mislabel token (mirrors the `tests/test-92-rename-no-long-leaves.cjs` idiom). `.planning/seeds/SEED-007-version-dynamic-first-touch-greeting.md` gained a `## Scope updates` section dated 2026-05-10 (Phase 95.6 D-07) recording the pattern-3 addition, the D-06 trigger, the scanner + test paths, and the pattern-2 scoping rationale; SEED-007's `status`/`binds_to_phase`/`binds_to_milestone` were not touched.
