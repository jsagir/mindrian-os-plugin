W2: give the investment assertion a real trigger, fix the write-read disagreement that would make
every new user's archetype wrong, and flip the GAP I-1 pin.

Purpose: GAP I-1 is the audit's finding that the FIRST session has zero investment ask. CONTEXT.md
D-04 locks the write to the FIRST_INSTALL conversation itself, before any room exists. The D-05
correction and research finding C-1 establish that this is a TRIGGER task, not a writer task: Phase

a second writer. Research finding C-3, reproduced empirically, established that

three-way tie that resolves to `student`. This phase is what makes that latent bug fire, so fixing it
is in scope, not a nice-to-have.

Decision D-L pins the ordering: reward strictly before investment, enforced in the router's state
machine and test-pinned, because getting the order wrong reproduces the exact defect the phase exists
to fix.

Output: the router's investment leg, the reader fix, the roundtrip pin, and the flipped GAP I-1 leg.
</objective>
