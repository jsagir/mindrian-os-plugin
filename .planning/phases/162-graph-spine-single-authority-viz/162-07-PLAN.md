<objective>
W7 of Phase 162 (the Phase 161 on-ramp): provide the seam where embedding-based
semantic clustering over the SAME room.db node ids supersedes literal co-occurrence
(W5) as the cross-concept signal, delivered as an OPTIONAL, NON-AUTHORITATIVE,
source_type-tagged meaning overlay that never persists as authoritative graph truth.
This plan builds the seam + the handoff contract; the embedding/vector substrate
itself is Phase 161 (explicitly OUT of scope per 162-CONTEXT).

Purpose: literal co-occurrence (a term in 2+ files) cannot surface implicitly
related, non-co-occurring concepts; embeddings can (the GraphRAG shape, research
Section 4). The on-ramp lets Phase 161's embedding layer plug into the single
identity space this phase established, without making the overlay a second
authority (Canon Part 9: room.db is the write-model truth; overlays are disposable
read-model projections).

Output: the optional overlay primitive (embedding-ready, degrades to co-occurrence),
the Phase 161 handoff contract, and tests. Hands off to Phase 161.
</objective>
