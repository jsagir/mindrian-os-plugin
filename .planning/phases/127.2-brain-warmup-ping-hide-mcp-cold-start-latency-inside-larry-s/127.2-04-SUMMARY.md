## Strategic significance of Instance #7 (cadence-problem moat now active in release pipeline)

This beta IS the hinge. Every prior beta this session (26, 28, 30) may have been sitting in tester caches without activating; "I tested beta.N" reports may have been against an older version. Plan 04's Class N `activation-reached-the-wire` acceptance gate -- now wired into release.sh Step 9.8 (post-publish full acceptance check) -- structurally closes that class of failure. The release pipeline now self-tests that the bytes it ships actually reach the wire on user machines. From v1.13.0-beta.32 forward, every beta either (a) activates on the wire AND earns the acceptance pass, or (b) fails the gate AND is prevented from shipping a phantom version. The cadence-vs-validation trade-off Jonathan named earlier in this session ("you shipped 7 builds in 2 hours, no tester validation") becomes structurally enforced rather than relying on manual maintainer discipline. Per Canon Part 6 (Product-as-Venture / dog-fooding mandate), the release script now honors its own canon: it self-tests that its own output actually activates on users' machines. The moat is the activation gate, not any individual fix.
