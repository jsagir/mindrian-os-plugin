### Architectural note (no deviation)

The plan's Step 2 directs passing `sentence: conversationSeed` into the closeOffer call in intent-classifier. closeOffer is NOT called inline in the hook -- the hook renders the F.1 selector (closer.renderF1) for the next-turn pickup, and the consumer surface (Larry on CLI/Desktop/Cowork) calls closeOffer when the user picks. The seam where the user sentence is in hand (conversationSeed) is therefore threaded onto the shared context and carried on the persisted F.1 payload (`f1Payload.sentence`), so the next-turn closeOffer receives it. This honors the plan's intent (classify where the text lives; only the scalars travel onward) while matching the shipped two-turn render/route split. The dial-close-reach.cjs defer/reject path has no user sentence in hand and stays on the no-sentence path, as the plan specifies.
