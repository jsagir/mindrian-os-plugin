## Executive summary

What a person can now do that they could not before this phase:

- Theo answers plain methodology questions. A question such as "how do I decide which customer segment to pursue first" reaches Theo on ask, search and query and the hook with one verdict. Anything that names the room's own people, ventures or private details is still refused, and the refusal says what kind of detail it was.
- The web search lines carry the room's own words. After one approval per run, on a card that lists every exact search string, the words leave as written. The closure test sends "Nimbus Robotics", "Orla Venn PhD" and "Haifa" from a quick run, a deep run and the Eureka perspective, and each leaves unchanged (MEASURED-34, closure leg WL).
- Every perspective searches. Eureka, analogies, whitespace, HSI, bottlenecks and find-connections each propose searches from the room's own titles. The four commands that used to be plan templates only (find-bottlenecks, find-analogies, find-connections, scout hsi) now carry a route through the planner.
- Every run says what it did not do. Each promised search is an operation with one of four end states (ran with results, ran empty with a counted zero, refused before any fetch, not executed). The answer names the questions that were not searched and why, says what an empty result does and does not prove, and says whether the searches against the claim ran. A run is "complete" only when its own ledger says so.
- The falsification pass has its own budget. A deep run sets aside a share of the search cap (3 of 16, 2 of 8, none at 1) that rounds cannot spend.
- Searches are built by practice, mechanism and neighbor field, not only by problem words. A bounded field scan runs first, and a limiter the field already overcame is demoted.
- Sentences are shaped, never quoted whole. A limiter written as a sentence is refused until it is named in a few words.
- Eureka says why nothing was judged. The patent lens searches patents or says it cannot. Missing providers are named before a run and in doctor.
- Cards and answer lines name the job and the move. No planner card or answer line in the closure runs carries an internal id (MEASURED-34, closure leg LINT: 30 texts, 535 lines, 0 violations).
