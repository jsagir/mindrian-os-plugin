## 2. Claims requiring correction or qualification

**C01. A tool's advertised label becomes a capability promise.**

Address: derive capability claims from provider mapping, credentials, topic constraints, and a preflight result. A label in documentation is insufficient.

**C02. A proposed repair is sold before its dependency is checked.**
Claim: SAPPhIRE encoding "turns structural: 0 into a working matcher" and is "the only way" the engine will reach the new front. S57.
Correction: the encoding is filed and the matcher rerun. Candidate and structural counts remain zero. The displayed implementation uses shared entity and framework nodes; Larry admits the prose/encoding diagnosis was wrong. S57-L5690.
Effect: O: an artifact and rerun fail to deliver the promised engine repair. The encoding may have separate analytical value, which Larry later describes; that does not validate the original repair promise.

**C03. Empty retrieval is promoted into evidence absence.**
Claim: "the scholarly corpus does not hold these questions"; "the range band, is unverifiable in scholarly literature." S57, L7344.
Missing support: a few exact-phrase searches establish their returns, not the contents of an entire corpus. This session also exposes sentence-shaped terms, dropped slots, and a relevant optical term never dispatched. Larry himself says he "wrote a search term that returned zero and called it an absence." S57, L7368.

## 7. Qualifications that must survive consolidation

- Counterevidence: beta.55's zero additional searches were corrected to an already-completed pass. Beta.57's missing-pass reports require their own per-operation verification. "Executed: 0" alone cannot settle either case.
- Canonical status: zero confirmed typed claims does not mean no evidence or no useful work. The review reports quote-verified rows, proposed claims, and confirmed whitespace nodes. These are different objects.
- Counts: S57/L6306 mentions 31 governed searches; its report and later review use 24 across the two deep runs. The counting windows or definitions are not reconciled in the supplied exports. Recompute from unique executed query IDs before publishing an exact efficiency comparison; do not infer fabrication from the discrepancy.
- Autonomy: the wait for 20 agents and quota failures do not establish that all 20 belong to the same task or prove the absence of admission control. The scheduler, account usage, and task tree must be inspected.
- Evidence quality: Larry often acknowledges uncertainty, stops invalid operations, admits mistakes, and revises work after counterexamples. Preserve those behaviors. The defect is inconsistent calibration before and between corrections, not an established intent to deceive.
- Guards: the source reports successful quote checks, plan validation, expired-grant refusal, and a verification-floor refusal, REV-L326. One working guard does not establish a working retrieval pipeline, but a retrieval failure does not justify removing the guard.
- Completeness: these exports omit some runtime material and truncate ordinary tool payloads. They support the quoted behavior and visible effects. They cannot prove every current code root cause, every external fact, or what an omitted operation did.

The entries in this annex remain proposed audit findings. Saving this report does not confirm or file claims in a MindrianOS Data Room.
