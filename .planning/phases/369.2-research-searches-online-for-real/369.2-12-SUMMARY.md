## C1 / C4: why the test changed and not the composer

The failing arms wanted `jobs.quick.lines` to hold `"Orla Venn PhD cold storage"` as a line of its own. The composer holds it inside `"Orla Venn PhD cold storage" OR "off-grid cold room for produce" OR "evaporative cooling for fresh produce"`. Question L2 runs under lens `ws.covered_elsewhere`, whose first template (lib/core/research-planner/families.cjs lines 95-97) is `ws.synonym_cover`: the quoted term followed by its quoted synonyms joined with OR. There is no `ws.exact` template on that lens, so the bare line never exists. Against the ruling: the person's words leave whole, quoted and unfiltered as the first alternative; nothing is stripped, filtered or reworded. The test now uses a `carries(list, phrase)` helper (dated comment 2026-10-05) for `q.lines`, the replay log, the audit `q` field and the plan's composed strings. The bar is still the whole quoted phrase.
