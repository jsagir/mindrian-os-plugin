## Risks

- A quick plan whose first leaf carries 3 or more kinds uses the whole cap of 3 on that leaf, and later leaves get no search (their op is no_query_composed). Plan 22 owns ordering.
- shapeWebPhrase uses contentTokens, which drops tokens shorter than 3 characters unless they hold a digit. A 2-letter acronym such as "UK" or "AI" is dropped from a shaped phrase.
- A 4-kind leaf adds up to 3 extra round-one searches per leaf in deep mode (the lane budget grows). The search cap of 16 still applies and closes the rest as search_cap.
- A kind value that cannot be shaped (fewer than 2 content tokens) refuses the whole leaf as bad_slot with slot name query_kinds:<kind>. The leaf goes local-only.
