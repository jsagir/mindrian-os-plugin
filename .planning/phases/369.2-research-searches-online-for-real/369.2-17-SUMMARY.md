## What changed

- CODE-05 (R10): `limiterSlot` in deep.cjs returns null when no leaf binds `slots.limiter`. `roundOneQueries` records such a lane in `out.refusals` and composes no query. `seedDeepLedger` closes each leaf of that lane as `refused_before_fetch` with reason `bad_slot:limiter`. `attachQueries` sets `leaf.refusal` on those leaves and keeps the lane in the plan. `prepareCounterevidence` composes a goal target or an assumed limiter only when `families.composableTerm` passes; otherwise it records the gap `bad_slot:goal` or `bad_slot:limiter`.
- CODE-06 (R11): `families.consumedSlots(lensId)` is new and exported. `composeForLeaf` refuses a leaf with `unused_slot` (code `unused_slot:<name>`) before it selects templates. `markLocalOnly` records `unused_slot:<slot>` and sets the plain leaf text for it. `planReviewCard` prints `not searched: <reason words>` through `job-lines.reasonWords` for a leaf with `leaf.refusal`.
