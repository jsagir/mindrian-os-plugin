## Ruling 2026-10-05 (navigator, verbatim: "grant WAHT IS GRANT ? CUZ RESAECH IS MUCH MORE THEN JUST GRANTS" ... "RENAME IT, TO A JTBD STATEMTN")

The word "grant" never reaches a user. It collides with the Opportunity Bank's funding grants and it is an internal name (INPUT addendum 2: jobs and moves, never ids). On every surface a navigator reads (the approval card's header and body, Larry's answer lines, the CLI output, research.md and its mirror, the research_run tool's next_step texts, the real-room run report) the approval is named by its job:

- Card header: **See every search before it leaves, then approve this run once.**
- Card body, first line: "These N searches will leave this machine for <the job in the navigator's words>. Nothing is sent until you approve." Then the exact strings, one per line.
- Answer line after approval: "Approved: N searches left for <job>." After refusal: "Nothing left this machine."
- Prose noun when one is unavoidable: "the search approval" (never "the grant").

The code keeps its identifiers (`grant_request`, `grants.cjs`, `grant_id`, the audit ledger's fields); only user-facing text changes. Owners: plan 09 (the card it builds now), plan 10 (CLI and MCP answer texts it moves), plan 11 (research.md, eureka.md, mirrors), plan 16 (job-lines.cjs sentences), plan 30 (the lint: zero occurrences of the word "grant" in any card or answer line; the lint lists the surfaces), plan 32 (the real-room run report).
