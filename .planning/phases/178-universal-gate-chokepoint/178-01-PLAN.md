<objective>
Extend the EXISTING CIRS coverage ledger with a second render_coverage column (C-1) -- one
ledger, two columns, not a parallel registry.

Per the navigator's two-plane framing (docs/CANON-PART-11-RENDER-TWIN-PROPOSAL.md "The two
planes - one discipline"): a gate-surfacing capability lives on BOTH planes. Plane 1 is
INVOCATION (does Larry reach the right thing -- CIRS R2/R9, already born-wired in
data/connector-coverage-ledger.json). Plane 2 is RENDER (does the reached gate FIRE its card
-- R15, this phase). The cleanest expression is ONE surface ledger where each surface declares
its connector: block (invocation: WIRED/excluded) AND its render coverage (card-emission /
render-only-excluded). check-render-coverage.cjs (178-02) reads the SAME ledger that
build-connector-registry.cjs walks. This makes the two-plane symmetry STRUCTURAL and DISSOLVES
residual R-3 (registry-completeness drift): a surface cannot be present in one registry and
missing from the other when there is only one.

This is the load-bearing constraint of the whole phase (C-1, the first of the three
non-negotiables D-178-03): born-wired only holds if EVERY render surface is enumerated by
code, not by a list someone has to remember to update (R-3). The cure for the five-phase slip:
Phase 177 minted lib/hmi/dial-selector.cjs F.7-dial with NO askuserquestion_marker and nothing
failed, because there was no exhaustive walk to catch it. This plan adds that walk AS A COLUMN
on the existing ledger.

REJECTED ALTERNATIVE (recorded per D-178-07): a separate scripts/build-render-coverage.cjs +
data/render-coverage-ledger.json parallel registry. Rejected because two registries can drift
(a surface in one, missing from the other) -- which is EXACTLY R-3, the registry-completeness
SPOF. One ledger with two columns cannot drift from itself. The render predicate (178-02) still
ships as a distinct scripts/check-render-coverage.cjs gate (C-2 deliverable, RESEARCH sec 9),
but it READS the one shared ledger rather than walking a second registry.

Per D-178-02 this REUSES the existing CIRS generator (build-connector-registry.cjs) and the
Phase-177 dial surface (the newest render entry), it does not duplicate them (Part 7). Per
D-178-04 additive only: the render column is ADDITIVE to the ledger (mirroring how the Phase
172-13 `class` enum was added without touching the wired/excluded/gap counts); no
reach/posture/edge/node minted, Part 3 frozen contracts (MAX_K=3, DIAL_REACH_K=6, the 0.70/0.15
gate, the 6-reach bank, the glyphs) untouched, no Brain wire (Part 8).

Purpose: make the render plane a STRUCTURAL column on the one CIRS ledger so a future render
surface CANNOT escape and the two planes CANNOT drift (R-3 dissolved).
Output: the render_coverage column on build-connector-registry.cjs + data/connector-coverage-
ledger.json, a green tests/test-render-column-exhaustive.cjs, and the tests/run-all-178.sh
aggregator scaffold.
</objective>
