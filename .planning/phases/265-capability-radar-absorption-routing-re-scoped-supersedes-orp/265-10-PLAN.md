<objective>
Three defects in `/mos:file-meeting` that need no subagents and are cheaper than the
parallelization question they were found next to. The parallel extraction redesign is plan 265-19;
these three are its precondition and stand on their own.

1. THE DATE IS NEVER ASKED UP FRONT. The command infers metadata at Step 1 and only confronts the
   date at Step 4's `requireValidAt` gate (`:466-486`), which can return `GATE_BLOCK` and stop a
   write the navigator already watched being assembled. Asking at ingest costs one question and
   removes a late block.

2. THERE IS NO TRANSCRIPT LENGTH GUARD. Verified by grep across `commands/file-meeting.md` and all
   ten `references/meeting/*.md`: no word count, no turn count, no duration check, no chunking
   note, no context-budget note about the transcript. The ONLY context management in the entire
   meeting surface is about OTHER meetings (`cross-meeting-intelligence.md:121`, max 10 prior
   summaries). A 4-hour transcript degrades with no signal, and the failure looks like "Larry
   missed some claims" rather than "the pass was over budget".

3. `hitl_shape: F.8` IS DECLARED AND NEVER RENDERED. The frontmatter at `:7` says the extracted
   nuggets are routed as an independent set. Step 4's actual filing gate is ASCII prose:
   "File both decisions? [all / review individually / skip]" (`:427`). The shipped F.8 machinery
   (`lib/hmi/shape-f8-renderer.cjs` `renderShapeF8`, `MAX_TOGGLE_N = 4` with paging;
   `lib/workflow/f8-fanout-consumer.cjs` `consumeF8Fanout`) is never called. This is the exact
   declared-versus-rendered failure class Canon R16 exists to catch.

Purpose: the nugget routing table is a standing HARD RULE in this repo, and the one command whose
`hitl_why` is written about nuggets has no rendered equivalent of it.
Output: a date-first ingest, an honest length probe, and a real F.8 gate.
</objective>
