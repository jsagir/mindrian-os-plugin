<objective>
Convert `/mos:file-meeting`'s Claimify extraction from one unified in-context pass into MULTIPLE
PARALLEL PERSPECTIVE subagents, each seeing the FULL transcript, with a consolidation step that
deduplicates and reconciles before the existing nugget routing gate.

**The navigator settled the shape and the trade.** The deep-dive research recommended against a
CHUNKED fan-out on economics: the per-worker reference payload (about 8 to 10k tokens of
`segment-classification.md` plus `knowledge-typing.md` plus `section-mapping.md`) exceeds the
per-chunk transcript payload for a normal meeting, so total spend roughly triples. That analysis is
sound and it is about a DIFFERENT shape. A perspective fan-out over the whole transcript is bought
for RECALL and thoroughness, not for wall clock or token savings, and the navigator has explicitly
valued that. Record the cost honestly in the command rather than pretending it is free: total
tokens go UP by roughly the number of perspectives, and that is the accepted price.

The research also corrects the argument that is usually made against this and is mostly NOT
supported by the file: chunking would not destroy quality for passes 1 through 4. Speaker identity
is resolved upstream in Step 2 and passed in. Disambiguation is explicitly bounded to the prior 2
to 3 turns. Multi-type resolution and the CTA conditions probe are claim-local. What genuinely
needs the whole view is cross-claim edge minting (`REFINES` / `ROOT_CAUSES` / `INSTANTIATES`,
`:378-394`) and intra-meeting contradiction detection, and BOTH are naturally consolidation-stage
work over short claim texts. Perspective workers each hold the whole transcript anyway, so they
lose nothing there either.

Four constraints from the research that shape the design and are not negotiable:

- **Workers cannot own any gate.** Step 3 requires showing reasoning for EVERY classified atomic
  claim; Step 4 is confirm-then-file per batch with structured rejection capture; Step 4's date-sync
  gate can return `GATE_BLOCK`. A subagent cannot own any of these, so the fan-out is
  EXTRACTION-ONLY and returns structured data.
- **Workers must not write to room.db.** `lib/core/room-db.cjs:243-262` folds `timeout: 5000` into
  `DatabaseSync` precisely because a background worker and a live conversation can both hold write
  intent on the same WAL file, and Phase 236 had to build a busy-versus-mid-migration error
  classifier. N agents each calling `writeClaimNode` would multiply that contention for no benefit.
  The main thread writes.
- **Transcript prose is untrusted input.** `tests/test-part8-poison-transcript.cjs` exists because a
  transcript can carry a proprietary number, a PII-shaped token, a competitor name or an injection
  token. Fanning the raw transcript into N tool-capable contexts multiplies that surface, so workers
  get a read-only grant and an explicit no-write, no-gate, no-user-interaction contract.
- **The approval gate is preserved, not bypassed.** Consolidation FEEDS the F.8 nugget routing table
  that plan 265-10 wired; it does not replace it. Canon Part 9 is enforced at the writer:
  `writeClaimNode` mints `review_status: 'proposed'` unconditionally, the `DO UPDATE SET` clause
  excludes `review_status`, and `promoteNodeStatus` rejects a literal `larry`, `brain`, `system` or
  `assistant` with `agent_attribution_forbidden`. A subagent may mint proposed claims and cannot
  confirm one even if it tried.

Purpose: recall. One pass over a long meeting misses what only one lens would have noticed.
Output: five whole-transcript perspectives, one reconciled claim set, one unchanged human gate.
</objective>
