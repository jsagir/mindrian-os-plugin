WHAT THE COUPLING ACTUALLY IS. The four legs are analytically INDEPENDENT: `deep_research`,
`diffusion_timing`, `analogies` and `web_validation` are four framings of the same fixed
opportunity node, and no leg consumes another leg's analysis. The `prev` parameter threaded through
`onStep` carries provenance plus the offline-degrade and `engine_mode` stamping, not analytical
input. They nevertheless run in order because `runChain` couples them through CONTROL flow:
`quality_early_stop` ends the chain when a step returns LOW quality, and `gateFn` halts on posture
before the material filing step.

WHY THE COST GUARD IS THE WHOLE DESIGN PROBLEM. `quality_early_stop` is not a formality. This
command's own `hitl_why` commits that "Exploration spends navigator-controlled research cost", and
TWO of the four legs (`deep_research` and `web_validation`) are web legs riding the frozen
deep_research reach. Sequential execution with an early stop means a cold `deep_research` leg
returning `insufficient_evidence` does not spend the other three. A naive parallel fan-out spends
all four unconditionally. Removing the early stop without replacing the property would be a silent
cost regression, which is exactly what the locked decision forbids.

THE MECHANISM CHOSEN, and it preserves the property exactly rather than approximating it:
**probe-first**. Run `deep_research` ALONE first, evaluate its quality with the SAME test
`quality_early_stop` applies, and only then fire the remaining three CONCURRENTLY. A cold corpus
short-circuits before the other three legs are ever dispatched, so nothing is spent, which is the
identical cost outcome the sequential chain produced. The parallel win is real (three legs collapse
into one round trip instead of three) and the guard is not weaker, it is the same guard moved one
step earlier. A navigator who wants all four regardless sets the documented override.

CONSTRAINT, non-negotiable: `lib/core/chain-executor.cjs` is under an explicit zero-diff arm in
`tests/run-all-264.sh`, pinned to base SHA `c7c33eea449f6f227c4cfbb86f220acaac9b5ab8`. This plan
touches `explore-chain.cjs` and the command markdown only.

Purpose: ship the wall-clock win the navigator asked for without dropping the cost control that
made the sequential order worth keeping.
Output: a probe-first parallel pre-pass with a sequential fallback and a gated filing step.
