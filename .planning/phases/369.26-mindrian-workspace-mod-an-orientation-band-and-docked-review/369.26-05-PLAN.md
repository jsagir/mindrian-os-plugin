<objective>
Draw the orientation band for a bound room at the two three-row tiers (UI-SPEC 6.1, 6.3, 7.1 components, 10.1 to 10.3; the navigator's WIDE, NARROW-minus and MISSING DATA concepts; C-01, C-03, C-04, C-06, C-09, C-12, C-13, C-14, C-15, C-16; WS-06).

Decisions this plan fixes (Claude's discretion; UI-SPEC rulings preserved):
- Tier table (UI-SPEC 10.1): yield when hasSurvey or maxRows under 4; T3-wide at columns 84+ and rows 6+; T3-compact at 72 to 83 and rows 6+; T1 at columns 30 to 71, or at rows 4 or 5 with columns 30+; T0 at columns under 30 with rows 4+. `pickTier` is a pure function with a truth-table test; thresholds are written against props only (R-02: they hold whatever `maxRows` counts).
- Label bolding: a deck string such as B10 or B20 is one string; `splitLabel(s)` bolds the part up to and including the first colon-space and leaves the rest regular (Typography: the word that names a block is bold). B51 and B52 bold only at the limit state per UI-SPEC.
- The ten-cell bar is drawn only at T3-wide, not at 80 percent or more (the block turns yellow, and blocks never nest; the words B52 already carry the message), and not in plain mode. Filled count is `Math.round(percent / 10)`; cells are `reading` (cream) under 50 percent and `yourMove` (yellow) from 50; an unfilled cell is a dim U+00B7 `Text` because a 1-column Box cannot carry an outline (UI-SPEC 7.1 ContextTile, R-14).
- The version tile is never drawn (C-10, OQ-04 default: no verified source, R-07).
- Row structure: root Box `flexDirection="row"` with `width` equal to `bodyColumns`; left the logo (fixed width), right a column of three `height={1}` rows; blocks are separated by one-cell `frame` Boxes; place, purpose and next use `flexGrow`; waiting and context and health use `flexShrink={0}`; every text uses `wrap="truncate-end"` (UI-SPEC 6.1 Proportions, item 12 of 17.2).
- `BandSlots = { row1Fix?: node; row2Fix?: node; row3Right?: node }`: empty here; plan 08 fills them with the Save, checkup and key-hint buttons.
