## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking, ENGINE-RULES wins] Fetchers take `LiveIo`, not `$`**
- **Issue:** the plan's `fetchX($)`, `resolveDirs($)` and `refreshViewModel($)` are all `$` across an import, which the engine scan refuses (ENGINE-RULES 1), and `update($, viewModelAtom, ...)` needs a literal reference in the file that holds `$` (rule 2).
- **Fix:** fetchers and `refreshViewModel` are pure over a `LiveIo` (src/model/live/io.ts). `registrars/model.ts` builds it (`makeIo($)`) with each `$.noun.event` and each env name as a string literal, and writes the model with `update($, { plugin, key: 'viewModel' } as const, ...)`. Proven with the real validate (hooks and calls listed "via makeIo"). As a benefit every fetcher is tested directly with a recording stand-in, and one arm drives the registrar through `$.session.start` and `$.turn.complete` with the real engine, intercepting `state.set`.
- **Effect on later plans:** plans 12 and 13 reuse `resolveDirs(io)` / `locate(io)` from binding.ts with a LiveIo; plans 10 and 14 refresh by registering their own hook with the same `makeIo` recipe (it is 10 lines in registrars/model.ts), never `refreshViewModel($)`.
