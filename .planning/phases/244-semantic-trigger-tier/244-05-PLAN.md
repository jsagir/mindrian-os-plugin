<objective>
Close the core of TRIG-01: a NEW sensor that mints a candidate reach by querying the ALREADY-SHIPPED `tri-modal-index.lexicalSearch`.

The gap this closes, precisely: `trigger_tier` is DECORATIVE today. It is computed once in `normalizeTurn` and stamped onto a copy of the turn; 2 of the 17 registered sensors copy it into their evidence bag; ZERO consumers gate, score, rank or filter on it. Its own doctrine comment says so at `sensor-types.cjs:72`. Adding the string `'content'` to `TRIGGER_TIERS`, which Plan 01 did, satisfies the letter of the requirement and changes nothing at runtime. TRIG-01 needs a real sensor, and this is it.

Every existing keyword sensor matches turn text against its OWN hardcoded vocabulary. Surveyed all 17: `sensor-diffusion-adoption` has 8 keyword constructs and 0 db reads, `sensor-room-pick` 7 and 0, `sensor-show-share` 7 and 0, `sensor-expert-skill` 2 keyword constructs and 1 db read that counts CONFIRMED SyntheticExpert nodes. NOT ONE queries a corpus. The content tier is a genuinely new capability, purely additive, and no sensor retires or merges.

Purpose: give free-form natural language a way to reach the right candidate when it carries no structural state-change signal and matches no sensor's private word list, but IS genuinely about material the room already holds.

Output: one new three-layer sensor file, one producer block inside `decide()`, three mechanical registry edits, one test file. No new npm dependency, no embedding call, no network, no new FTS table.

Zero-hit floor discipline, measured, not guessed: against the real `rethinking-mindrianos` room with `fragments` EXCLUDED and only 1229 curated `claim` nodes in the corpus, the turns "what is the weather in paris today", "my cat needs a vet appointment tomorrow" and "order pizza for dinner tonight" each produced 0 hits, while "the reverse salient in the graph derivation pipeline" produced 5 hits at 40 percent token coverage and "opportunity bank agentic reasoning environment" produced 5 hits at 100 percent coverage. A clean natural zero-hit floor appears with NO threshold tuning. The corpus choice does almost all the work.
