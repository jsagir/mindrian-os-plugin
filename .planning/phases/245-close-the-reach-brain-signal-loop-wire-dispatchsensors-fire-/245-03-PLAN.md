<objective>
Root-cause and fix the Part 8 egress guard's over-fire on contentless Brain calls (Requirement 5,
D-26, D-27), and document the adjacent `brain_search` finding without widening the boundary
(D-28).

Root cause is 245-RESEARCH.md F-4 (the terminal catch-all), and F-5 establishes the defect class
is wider than `brain_stats`: `brain_schema` is equally blocked and `pws-brain.md`'s documented
`brain_search` fallback is dead.

Purpose: `lib/core/part8-egress-guard.cjs::classify()` has three positive recognizers (the
CONTENT-SET forbidden-pattern scan, the typed MOVE-SET packet shape, and the free-form
vocabulary match for `brain_ask` / `brain_query`) and one terminal catch-all returning
`ambiguous` at line 268. `scripts/part8-egress-guard-hook.cjs` turns `ambiguous` plus
`brainAvailable()` into `exit 2`, a block. A contentless `{}` carries nothing for any recognizer
to match, so it lands in the catch-all and blocks. Reproduced live across 8 payload/tool
combinations: `classify({}, {toolName: 'mcp__plugin_mos_mindrian-brain__brain_stats'})` returns
`{verdict:'ambiguous', class:'unknown', reason:'neither proven move-set nor content hit'}`, while
`classify({question:'lean startup methodology'}, {toolName: '...brain_ask'})` returns `allow`.

The guard's risk ordering is therefore INVERTED: a free-form call carrying a real user question
is allowed, while a zero-argument stats read that cannot possibly leak is blocked. The verdict is
over-firing, not correctly conservative. `lib/core/brain-client.cjs:575` calls
`callTool('brain_stats', {})` and `:488` calls `callTool('brain_schema', {})`, both literal empty
objects, and `hooks/hooks.json:236`'s matcher
`mcp__(?:plugin_[a-z0-9_-]+_)?mindrian-brain__.*` catches both.

Canon Part 8 is NOT reopened by this plan. The fix narrows what is ambiguous; it never widens
what is allowed to carry content. The catch-all's fail-closed posture is untouched.

Output: one positive recognizer, one test, one RCA document.
</objective>
