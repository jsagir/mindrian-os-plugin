`sensorCtx` at `lib/core/navigation-engine.cjs:876`. Near-zero new plumbing.

`sensor-memory-cortex.cjs:84` already fires `cross_room` on `freshContradictions > 0`, so a hats
sensor on the same field at `> 0` would double-fire on every single contradiction. One fresh
contradiction is a memory-cortex bridge; two or more unresolved contradictions are a perspective
lock worth a hats rotation. That threshold is also a Canon Part 12 invisibility restraint, not
merely a de-duplication fix.

1 percent chance" rule. It does not transfer, for three independent and each-sufficient reasons:

interruption rather than one file read. SENS-17 stays a deterministic threshold sensor.
