Merge the fused signal into the dial's score map at the render callsite, which is the ONLY seam
that actually reaches what the navigator picks from, and close the pre-existing telemetry
divergence in the same change (D-01 corrected plug point per F-1, D-02, D-03, D-04, and
245-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 7 / Open Question 2).

Purpose: `scripts/intent-classifier.cjs:1274` builds `reachScores` from
`cortex-reach-adapter.buildReachScoresFromCortex(cortexNodes)`, folds the Phase 158-03 reject
discount over it with `Object.assign` at :1288, applies the relevance gate at :1307, and hands
the composed map to the pure `buildReachList` at :1341. That `Object.assign` fold is the shipped,
tested precedent for injecting a second signal at exactly this seam, and it is where the fusion
belongs. `buildReachList` stays byte-unchanged (D-03): it is contractually pure and receives only
`{tierMode, reachScores, suppressedReachIds}`, so merging upstream changes its INPUT without
touching its code, and both purity tripwires stay green.

245-RESEARCH.md names the single most likely way this phase ships a false success: fusing inside
`rankFiredCandidates` and calling Requirement 1 done. Its warning sign is literally "a plan whose
R1 task list contains no edit to `scripts/intent-classifier.cjs`". This plan is that edit.

`buildReachList({tierMode, reachScores})` for the `reach_presented` telemetry with ONLY those two
keys. It already omits the Phase 158-03 discount merge, the `suppressedReachIds` set, and the
relevance gate the live render applies. D-04 named this prospectively as a hazard; research
confirmed it is already live today, and the fusion widens it. Open Question 2's recommendation is
to close it by giving both sites one shared composition helper. This plan takes that option rather
than documenting the debt.
