## Task 2: The Commit That Was Refused Once

### What was staged

Re-measured before staging rather than trusting the plan's numbers, because this is a shared
tree. `git status --porcelain -- commands/ skills/` returned exactly **34** files: 17 commands
and 17 mirrors. HEAD was `e428a8f1`, unchanged from the wave-4 close. The four untracked paths
(`docs/MINDRIANOS-PRD.md`, `docs/2026-08-20-gate0-queries.cypher`, `prototypes/`, `specs/`)
belong to another session and were not touched.

All 34 were staged **by explicit path**. Not `git add -A`, and the reason is specific:
`267.3-04-SUMMARY.md` recorded that the protection which had made these files safe to leave in a
shared tree was gone. The protection was the linter block itself - any session trying to commit
them hit the same gate - and applying the declarations dissolved it. That is the generalizable
lesson from this wave: **a gate that happens to hold uncommitted work in place is not a safety
mechanism, it is a blocker whose removal silently removes the safety too.**
