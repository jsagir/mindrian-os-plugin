Thread the room scope through every consumer of `rs_cache.py` and close the temporary
empty-external-corpus state plan 296-04 deliberately left open.

This plan is where the repoint becomes real for users. It also carries the phase's most dangerous
class of edit: renames whose reader and writer live in different files. 296-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4
documents the exact failure shape from a prior audit, where a dependency edge broke and the failure
was silent because the consumer degraded instead of erroring. Three renames in this plan have that
property. Each one MUST land with its readers in the same commit.

The most subtle item is the SEED-018 semantic gate. `_gate_records_pinecone` builds its topic vector
"from the SAME Pinecone inference" as the record vectors it gates. After the repoint the records
carry 384-dim local vectors. Left pointing at Pinecone inference, that gate either mixes a 1024-dim
topic vector against 384-dim records (the exact anti-pattern this phase exists to prevent) or
silently no-ops and stops filtering off-topic documents from the differential. Neither failure
raises. Both change scoring quietly.

Output: five wired files, one updated sibling test, and a zero-key end-to-end fence.

## Open Question 5 resolved: name the auto-explore dispatch leftover, do not fix it here

296-RESEARCH.md Open Question 5 asked whether `scripts/auto-explore-fire.cjs`'s direct
`python3 scripts/rs-engine.py --mode hybrid` spawn should be moved onto the
`lib/core/rs-backend-dispatch.cjs` chokepoint in this phase, and recommended "at minimum include it
in the blast radius." This plan NAMES it and FENCES it, and deliberately does NOT complete the
Phase 272-10 wiring. The navigator can override; the reasoning is stated here so an override is a
decision rather than a discovery.

Three reasons:

1. **It would regress hybrid mode, not fix it.** `resolveBackend()` returns `'cjs'` by default, and
   `lib/core/rs-engine.cjs` implements Mode A internal ONLY. Routing a `--mode hybrid` spawn through
   the chokepoint today would land hybrid on a backend that cannot serve it. Completing 272-10 for
   this caller requires `rs-engine.cjs` to grow Mode C first, which is Phase 283 or a Phase 272
   follow-up, not this phase.
2. **It would change another phase's acceptance state from inside this one.**
   `lib/core/rs-backend-dispatch.cjs` lines 38-44 record that `tests/272-dispatch-chokepoint.sh` and
   `tests/272-rule6-amended.sh` "stay RED until that wiring lands." Turning them green here makes
   both phases' verification ambiguous: a reader cannot tell whether 272 was completed or 296
   overreached.
3. **The actual risk is silence, and silence is closable without the rewiring.** Pitfall 5 notes
   that this spawn's failure is swallowed into `markFailed(..., 'all_pipelines_empty')` telemetry,
   so a break shows up as a slow drift in `auto_explore_skipped` rather than an error. Task 3 closes
   that with a source comment plus an argv-contract test asserting that every flag
   `auto-explore-fire.cjs` passes is still accepted by `rs-engine.py`'s parser.

What this plan therefore does to `scripts/auto-explore-fire.cjs`: adds a block comment and NO
behavior change. What it adds elsewhere: `tests/296-blast-radius.test.cjs`, which fails loudly if
the argv contract between the two files ever drifts.
</planner_decision>
