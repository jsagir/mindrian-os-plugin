the one thing no test in this phase can.

recorded that exact silent-degrade class for the whitespace pipeline. CONTEXT.md D-06 therefore
locks the scope: `PINECONE_API_KEY` and the `pinecone` package STAY, because
`scripts/compute-hsi.py` Tier 2 and `lib/core/pinecone-inference.cjs` are both still load-bearing
and both explicitly out of scope.

assert PRESENCE, because an over-eager cleanup is the more likely failure here than a missed
removal.

Tier 2 as the one behavior that cannot be automated within this phase's scope, and because two
navigator-facing decisions were resolved on the planner's own authority and deserve an explicit
ratification rather than a silent pass-through.

Purpose: fence the boundary in both directions, document the new seams, close the phase honestly.

and a signed-off manual check.
