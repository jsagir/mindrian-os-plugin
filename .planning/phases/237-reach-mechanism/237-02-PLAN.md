<objective>
Collapse `chain_run`'s private autonomy classifier into the authority the rest of the repo already uses, so `framework_run` and `chain_run` cannot disagree about what is material.

Purpose: this is not a data drift, it is a category error. `framework_run` reads `data/command-registry.json` `commands[].autonomous_safe` (an autonomy flag). `chain_run` reads `data/connector-registry.json` `connectors[].posture === 'push_forward'` (a pedagogical reach dial from the frozen-3 vocabulary). Measured live during research: 48 of 112 registered commands disagree, and 12 disagree in the dangerous direction where `chain_run` auto-runs unattended a step `framework_run` gates, including `/mos:new-project`, `/mos:ignite` and `/mos:pipeline`. `commands/ignite.md` line 43 literally says "every birth step is forced-material, nothing auto-runs" while `chain_run` classifies it autonomous_safe.

`lib/core/recipe-maps.cjs::postureForCommand` already IS the declared "ONE posture authority" and is already the sole reader for 7 of the 8 consumers in the repo. Canon Part 7 makes this a delete-and-point, not a build. Do not create a third module.

Output: `chain.cjs` with roughly 45 lines deleted and one default removed, a full-registry parity gate, a structural source fence, and the existing halt regression retargeted in the same change.
</objective>
