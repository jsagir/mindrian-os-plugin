<objective>
Close the data-safety gap that plan 386-45 left (its SUMMARY, section Risk left): repairRoomIdentity (lib/core/navigation/room-identity.cjs) renames room.db aside on ANY open failure once a recovery card is approved (moveAsideUnreadable, called from _recoverIdentity of lib/core/graph-backfill.cjs). A false unreadable, for example a transient Windows lock where SQLITE_CANTOPEN is raised for a healthy file, could therefore move a healthy room.db aside. Plan 386-45 added the typed cause (busy, io, cannot_open, not_a_database, other); this plan uses it.

Part 9: room.db is the local mind; the open path and its migration safety stay as they are. Part 8: no egress. Reproduce on SYNTHETIC fixtures only: a healthy synthetic room.db plus a double of openRoomDb that throws the simulated error. Never read, copy, open or touch a real room.
Output: RCA with the repro, a failing test, the guard by cause at the origin, the readiness change that stops the card from offering the move-aside.
</objective>
