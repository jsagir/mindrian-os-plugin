<objective>
Diagnose the over-blocking of ordinary Theo and context questions (EGRESS-CLAIMS-CHECK.md claim e, CONFIRMED; the rest from section F of the findings source) and get the owner decision on the exact rule changes. Three causes: (1) the capital-letter name rule blocks authors, companies, months, places and the plugin's own nouns (Larry, Data Room) while the lower-case form passes; (2) ordinary business words sit on the default-deny list (runway, ARR, standup, meeting with, 80 percent, city names); (3) my or our questions and an unreadable room.db block almost every free-form question, and the unreadable-database result is cached until the files change.

Owner direction (2026-11-06, PRD section Owner direction on ambiguous calls and over-blocking), for this plan, verbatim: "follow last asnwer for this one as well". The last answer (plan 26) is: "block ? i dont think a block will wor it need to be context based, like the file-a meeting style where it braeks it up to claims, facts hypthtesesys ect ...". So this plan does not choose "narrow rules only". The decision is option-e: the context-based split route of plan 386-42. The narrowing of the name and deny-word rules stays only for sprouts, the part the route needs (a short keyword query must pass); an edge or a body that fails a prose rule, and a short private claim, are not refused and forgotten: the guard answers them with the typed outcome sprouts_required and the host model splits them (plans 386-27 and 386-33).

Rules: narrow the rules for sprouts, do not delete protection. Target design: the sprout/body shape test (plan 386-37, lib/core/part8-sprout-shape.cjs) and the split route (plan 386-42). A clear sprout (a short keyword query) skips the word-list rules (name rule, deny-list words, city names, money and percent words) and passes; credentials and structured identifiers (email, phone-like, SSN-like, artifact-style ids) always block; a body, an edge that fails a prose rule and a sprout that holds a private-claim marker (a my or our possessive, a first-person statement, a private word such as confidential) are answered sprouts_required; a room-lexicon term blocks. A word list that blocks an ordinary keyword is the wrong tool. This plan runs after plan 386-31 (key shapes) because both change the same pattern files, and after plan 386-42 (the route and the outcome). The owner decision is recorded (option-e); the plan stops with NEEDS_HUMAN only if the RCA shows the design cannot work (for example the route cannot carry a needed sprout without room text).
</objective>
