<objective>
Build the shape test the owner principle asks for, as a pure helper with no verdict change yet.

Owner principle for Part 8 (2026-11-06, PRD section Owner principle for Part 8): a specific keyword query for research or for the remote graph may leave the room; a full body of text may not; small pieces (sprouts) go out and MindrianOS assembles the answer; the guard must never cripple contextualised work, query or search. The guard separates sprouts from bodies by shape (size, sentences, quoted room text) and always blocks credentials. A word list that blocks an ordinary keyword is the wrong tool.

The guard has no shape test today: it uses word lists on ordinary keywords (an author, a company, a month, a place, runway) and so over-blocks short queries, while a pasted body of text is only caught when it happens to hit a word. This plan measures the shape of the corpora, fixes thresholds, and ships the helper. The helper has three classes: sprout (a short keyword query), body (a paragraph, a quoted passage, several lines) and edge (everything between, for example a first-person statement). Plan 386-32 asks the owner where the line goes; plans 386-25, 386-27 and 386-33 use the helper.
Output: RCA with measurements, a failing test, the module.
</objective>
