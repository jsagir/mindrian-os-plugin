<objective>
Migrate the Stop-gate enforcement LAST (D-05), and ONLY after the server-side gate dedup + relevance machinery exists. First build `lib/mcp/gate-dedup.cjs` (fire a gate ONCE, only when relevant) and `lib/mcp/stop-gate-handler.cjs` (the daemon-side host that receives the moved enforcement and wires `shouldFireGate` into gate_render's fire decision), then move the Stop-gate enforcement server-side so `scripts/on-stop` (the real Stop surface: 20KB bash, dispatched via `hooks/run-hook.cmd on-stop` at hooks.json:142, invoking lib/core business modules directly) becomes a thin wake+render adapter. The card-misfire regression class the navigator logged all week must be FIXED by this move, never re-created by it.

Purpose: The Stop-gate is the highest-risk hook to move because a bad move re-creates the per-turn card noise (Pitfall 7, D-05). This plan is deliberately sequenced last: the gate ladder (Plan 05) and the thin-adapter shape (Plan 08) must be proven first, and the dedup machinery must exist before the enforcement moves.
Output: lib/mcp/gate-dedup.cjs, lib/mcp/stop-gate-handler.cjs, the thin scripts/on-stop adapter, the extended adapter-budget audit covering the Stop surface.

D-05 is a hard sequencing constraint: Stop-gate does NOT move until gate dedup + relevance exist. D-04/Pitfall 7: the gate fires ONCE per session on genuine relevance only. D-06: the migrated Stop hook invokes zero lib/core business modules on the flag-ON path. The frozen constitutional floor (MAX_FORCE_RETRIES=3, MAX_SESSION_INTERCEPTS=12, held in scripts/check-card-fire.cjs) stays byte-untouched.
</objective>
