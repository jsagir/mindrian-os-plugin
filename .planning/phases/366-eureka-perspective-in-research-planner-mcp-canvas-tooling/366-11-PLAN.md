<objective>
Close the vocabulary gap through Theo, gated per term and intent-led, under a navigator yes.

Purpose: D-13 (navigator ruling 2026-10-01: "the guard can change! ... the intent and context must
lead not just names"). 80.2% of 355's pairings never asked Theo because a handle did not resolve.
A miss now offers its term on the F.8 card; a yes sends exactly that term, with its intent
envelope recorded, to Theo's `normalize_framework_name`; a hit becomes a proposed translation the
navigator confirms.

Two facts shape the wire (recorded here, also in the handoff):
- Theo's `normalize_framework_name` is a `z.strictObject` with the single key `raw`
  (/home/jsagi/Theo/src/mcp/content/normalize-framework-name.ts:118-147); unknown keys are refused
  before the handler runs. So today the wire payload is `{ raw: term }`; the full envelope
  `{ raw, intent, section, perspective }` is built, classified by the guard and recorded in the
  audit row, and the guard arm already accepts the envelope shape so Theo's intent-led resolver
  (filed as a Theo-side request by plan 366-24, D-13 c) can take it without another guard change.
- The audit ledger's key set is closed and `part8_verdict` accepts only `pass | tripped`, so the
  row records `part8_verdict: 'pass'` and the `navigator_released` reason lives in the guard
  verdict (PATTERNS discrepancy 2). No audit key is added.

Settled here: only carried `framework:` / `methodology:` values are ever offered for release,
never titles (a title is room prose; Part 8). The release runs through its own single-use gate,
minted per term at basket time and shown on the F.8 card, because filing.fileRun is synchronous
and the Theo call is not. The egress policy (plan 366-17) applies: if the `theo` line is off, the
offer is shown with "Theo is off in this room's egress policy" and no gate is minted.
Output: the guard arm, canon-release.cjs, basket items, CLI subcommands, two tests, a live check.
</objective>
