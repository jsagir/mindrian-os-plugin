The policy module contract these tests pin (360-07 implements it; planner decisions P-1 and P-2 below):
- `lib/core/room-bind-picker-policy.cjs` exports `cwdRoomsHomeVerdict(cwd, roomsRoot)` returning exactly one of `'inside'`, `'outside'`, `'ancestor'`, `'unresolvable'`, and `unboundPickerSuppression({binding, cwd, roomsRoot})` returning `null`, `'no-room-remembered'` or `'cwd-outside-unbound'`. Neither ever throws.
- `'unresolvable'`: cwd not a non-empty string, not absolute, realpath fails, or not a directory; or the rooms root cannot be resolved. `'inside'`: realpath(cwd) equals or sits under realpath(rooms root). `'ancestor'`: realpath(rooms root) sits under realpath(cwd). Else `'outside'`.
- P-1 (planner decision, N-1): an ancestor of the rooms home (for example `~` when the rooms home is `~/MindrianRooms`) is "ambiguous" and fires, because such a session may be doing room work; the snapshot shows 0 human pickers at an ancestor cwd, so this does not affect the R10 acceptance. The navigator can flip it later by changing one branch.
- P-2 (planner decision, N-2): "chose no room" means the binding has no real primary (primary empty or the sentinel) AND carries the sentinel as primary or in bound. A session whose primary is a real room keeps today's off-scope behavior even if bound also lists the sentinel. The sentinel value is read from `lib/core/session-binding.cjs` (`NO_ROOM_SLUG`, exported by 360-07), never re-typed.
- Unbound means no real primary, no real room in bound, and no sentinel.

R11 scope note (recorded, not silently dropped): the answer reaches the binding store through the existing typed-answer consumer (an exact option label on the next prompt). An answer given inside the AskUserQuestion card returns to the model as a tool result, and no hook captures it today (0 binding cards were answered in the snapshot). N-2 says "store it in the existing per-session binding store (whatever binds a room today)", so this phase honors every stored no-room choice and keeps the existing capture path; a card-answer capture is recorded as a follow-on candidate in 360-08.
