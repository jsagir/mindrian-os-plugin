## Decision map

See `275-01-PLAN.md`'s Decision map for the full D-01 through D-13 table. This plan implements the
mechanical consequence of **D-01** (section-set grows 8 to 11) in the two runtime files that carry
a hand-copied duplicate of the vocabulary. It introduces no new decision.

<objective>
Close the vocabulary drift the grown table exposes: two runtime modules carry hand-typed copies of
the eight-section list and both went stale the moment plan 275-01 landed.

Purpose: without this, `birthRoom()` writes 8 Section nodes into a room whose scaffold created 11
directories. More importantly, a hand-copied literal is exactly the propagation-gap disease this
phase's L3 work exists to catch (`domain-explorer`, `scenario-analysis`, `trending-to-absurd`,
`analyze-needs` are four instances of it already paid for). Fixing the citations while leaving a
live mirror in place would be a Canon Part 6 dog-fooding violation.

Output: zero hand-copied section-vocabulary literals under `lib/`, with every existing consumer
still working by name.
</objective>
