### D-11 rationale (a Claude's Discretion item, resolved here, on the record)

`275-CONTEXT.md` leaves one scope call open: whether dilutive/equity funding tracking gets BUILT
this phase, or whether the L2 contract documents the scope and defers the build. Resolved in favour
of **document now, build later**, for three reasons:

1. **Phase boundary.** Phase 275's boundary is the room SCHEMA by ICM layer. Building an equity/VC
   pipeline is a command-surface build in `commands/funding.md` plus whatever writes its state, a
   different kind of work with a different blast radius.
2. **No analog exists.** `275-PATTERNS.md`'s own "No Analog Found" table lists dilutive/equity
   tracking with a grep-confirmed zero hits for `equity`, `VC`, `loan`, `crowdfund` and `angel`
   anywhere in `commands/funding.md`. Building it means designing a second stage machine from
   scratch, not extending one.
3. **This is precisely what an L2 contract is FOR.** SEED-084's `## ADDENDUM 2026-09-04i` says it
   in as many words: "a documented gap between contract and code, which the whole point of writing
   L2 contracts is meant to catch and prevent next time, not repeat here on day one." A contract
   that names the full shape and marks half of it NOT YET BUILT does the job the layer exists to do.

This is **not** scope reduction dressed up. The contract ships the full Dilutive plus Non-Dilutive
shape the primary source defines; only the command-surface implementation of the dilutive half is
deferred, and it is deferred loudly, inside the artifact whose job is to surface exactly that.
The SUMMARY must register it as a named follow-up so it is discoverable, not lost.
