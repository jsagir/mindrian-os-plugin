# Phase 379.1 Plan 04: Drain embeds once, prunes by top-k cosine, walks a cursor, runs capped Summary

**The graph-derive drain now embeds each artifact once per content hash, scores at most N x k pruned pairs in slices of 5,000, writes its attempt before the work, runs at low priority under a PID-checked lock, and discloses what the artifact scope removed.**

## What the plan's output asks for (first)

- **Shipped DEFAULT_PRUNE_K: 20.** It equals `chosen_k` in `tests/fixtures/379.1/probe-k.json`.
- **recall_shortfall: false.** The probe status was `ok`; no RECALL_SHORTFALL comment was written.
- **Recall next to the pairs_pruned arithmetic (PROVEN from probe-k.json):**

| Leg | artifacts | all pairs | candidates at k = 20 | pairs the pruner skips | CONVERGES recall | INFORMS recall |
|-----|-----------|-----------|----------------------|------------------------|------------------|----------------|
| baseline (real room) | 39 | 741 | 190 | 551 | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| large sample (every 7th of 2158) | 300 | 44,850 | 977 | 43,873 | 1.0 | 0.9925 |

  The drain writes `pairs_pruned = all pairs - pairs_total`. For the 2,163-artifact incident entry the bound is min(2,163 x 20, 2,338,203) = 43,260 pairs, so `pairs_pruned` is at least 2,294,943. That number counts pairs not scored. It does not count edges lost. The edge loss is the recall column: about 0.75 percent of INFORMS edges on the large sample, none of the CONVERGES edges. At k = 10 the sample loses 14.6 percent of INFORMS edges, which is why k stays 20.
- **Measured save count on the 40-artifact cadence test: 2** (after batch 8 and at the end), with 10 encoder batches. Both `result.saves` and a spy on `vectorCache.save` read 2. The plan's limit was not 10.
- The ceiling of 50,000 pairs per entry is not reached at the incident size (43,260), so that entry runs through the cursor over 9 drains of 5,000 pairs or fewer, not through the skip. A room above about 2,500 artifacts hits the ceiling and clears with a disclosed `pair_cap_exceeded` skip.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The drain integration of test-379.1-vector-cache landed in the Task 2 commit, not Task 1**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the plan puts the drain-driven assertions in Task 1's test, but the drain is rewritten in Task 2. A Task 1 commit with a failing drain assertion would break the per-task green rule.
- **Fix:** Task 1 carries the classifier-level assertions (cadence, deadline, limit, provenance, attach counts). Task 2 adds the drain-driven check (8 texts, 0, 1).
- **Files modified:** tests/test-379.1-vector-cache.cjs
- **Commit:** e2c6541a2 and c3060173f

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Cached vectors would override an injected encoder seam**
- **Found during:** Task 1 design (read of `scoreBasedDeriveFn`: `opts.vectors` wins over `opts.encodeFn`)
- **Issue:** the plan calls `attachVectors` in the async backfill and the drain with no condition. With a caller-injected `probeOpts.encodeFn` and no `embedFn`, the real spine would attach vectors and override the stub, which breaks the 224 and 233 parity suites and loads the real model in tests.
- **Fix:** vectors are attached only when no seam is injected, or when the caller also supplies `embedFn`. A seam without `embedFn` scores the plain sequence, bounded by the cursor (drain) or the entry cap (backfill).
- **Files modified:** lib/core/graph-backfill.cjs, scripts/gsd-graph-derive-drain.cjs
- **Commit:** e2c6541a2, c3060173f

**3. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Progress guarantee under a tiny budget**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (scenario 8)
- **Issue:** a budget check before the first batch or first pair could make a drain do nothing, drain after drain.
- **Fix:** each drain embeds at least one batch and scores at least one pair; the deadline is checked after that. The pruner pass is still bounded because its deadline check is per row.
- **Commit:** e2c6541a2, c3060173f

**4. [Rule 2 - Missing critical] Cumulative edge count and an extra scope field**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** a multi-drain entry would report only the edges of its last slice; Plan 02 asked Plans 04 and 05 to expose `scope.stats.truncated`.
- **Fix:** `edges_written` is stored in the queue entry across drains; the events carry `scope_prewalk_truncated` next to the three planned scope fields.
- **Commit:** c3060173f
