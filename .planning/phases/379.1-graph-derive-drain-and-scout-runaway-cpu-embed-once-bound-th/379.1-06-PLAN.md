<objective>
Add the large-room leg to the real-room release run, gate the cut on it, document every new tunable and the leg itself, prove the shared scope on both walkers, and file the deferred follow-ups as seeds.

Purpose: RULE 10 says no cut without a real-room run read by a human. beta.73 must prove, on a room of more than 2,000 artifacts, that the drain worker and the scout stay inside their clock, CPU and RSS bounds. The fixture is built at run time (never 2,000 committed files). The gate refuses a receipt that lacks the leg or that ran the leg in smoke mode.

Output: the leg module, the real-room-run wiring and the --large-room-only flag, the gate verdicts, the docs section and the two RULE 10 paragraphs, the final assertions in two phase tests, and six seed files.

Decisions implemented here: D-379.1-02 (gate the release), D-379.1-12 (SimHash LSH escalation seed), D-379.1-17 (MRL seed), D-379.1-03 (Python walkers seed), D-379.1-16 (idle-only maintenance seed), D-379.1-14 (the scout cap rise after the randomized solver is a seed, main-session ruling in revision 1), the rank-fusion note in CONTEXT.md (RRF seed). D-379.1-05 (operator mitigation on the Windows machine) is a main-session action, not a task.

What the leg measures and what it does not (checker, revision 1): the scout child hits the artifact-cap envelope on purpose; above the cap the scout must not enter the LSA matrix (D-379.1-14 floor), so the leg proves the cap, the clock, the CPU and the peak RSS of that path. The LSA path at scale is measured by tests/test-379.1-svd-parity.cjs step 5 (2,105 x N under 60 s) when Plan 03 ships, and the step timeout by tests/test-379.1-scout-bound.cjs scenario (1); the leg does not repeat them.
