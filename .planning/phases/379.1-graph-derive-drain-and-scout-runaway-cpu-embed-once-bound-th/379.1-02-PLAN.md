Recorded deviation (checker, revision 1; accepted by the main session 2026-10-21): D-379.1-07 says one SQLite transaction per batch of vectors. This phase stores the vectors in the JSON file <room>/.mindrian/graph-derive-vectors.json in the byte-shape rs-engine already writes to .rs-engine-cache.json, rewritten atomically (tmp plus rename) as one unit. Reason: Canon Part 7 (reuse before build): rs-engine's cache format, its loader and its saver exist and are pinned by the 272 tests; a SQLite table would be a second cache format next to it. Cost control: the caller saves once per SAVE_EVERY_BATCHES = 8 encoder batches (256 vectors) and at the deadline, at an encoder failure and at the end, not once per batch; at batch 32 over 2,163 artifacts that is about 9 rewrites of a file of up to about 16 MB instead of 68. A crash loses at most 256 embeds (seconds of work). SQLite is not introduced this phase.
