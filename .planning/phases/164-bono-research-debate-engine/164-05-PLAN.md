- lib/core/graph-derivation.cjs::runDerivation({roomDir, runChain, selfCritiqueFn, deriveFn,
  artifactPairs, llm}) -> {proposedNodes, edges, trace} is the SHIPPED derivation loop. It drives a
  deriveFn (the producer) over the debate's artifact pairs as material:true steps, fable-mode-critiques
  each candidate via its OWN selfCritiqueFn, and writes the survivors as PROPOSED truth-claim NODES +
  typed edges through the navigation chokepoint, drawing edge_type ONLY from the frozen
  graph-derivation.CASCADE_SUBSET (CONTRADICTS / CONVERGES / INFORMS / INVALIDATES / ENABLES / REFINES /
  ROOT_CAUSES -- the v1.12 frozen members). runDerivation ACCEPTS the injected runChain composer (so
  each derived relationship is a material runChain step) and is idempotent (the GDH-07 stable node_seed
  guard). The debate's per-step derived relationships (a per-hat argument that produces a CONVERGES /
  CONTRADICTS / REFINES / ROOT_CAUSES claim about the hypothesis) ride runDerivation, NOT a bespoke
  loop. candidateToFinding(candidate) is the producer-tuple -> finding adapter the deriveFn emits.

- lib/core/findings-wirer.cjs::wireAccept(db, params) is the SHIPPED proposed-truth-claim-NODE-then-
  typed-EDGE writer: it mints an EvidenceClaim NODE (review_status proposed; NEVER confirmed -- Part 9
  role 5; the review_status lands on the NODE, not the edge) and writes the INFORMS edge to the
  resolved target, plus CONTRADICTS / SUPERSEDES on the decision flags, all through navigation.writeEdge
  + navigation.writeEvidenceClaim (the chokepoint). The ruling artifact + each residual-tension record
  route through wireAccept (with wireReject carrying the REJECT-with-reason as REJECTED_BECAUSE data,
  Part 4) -- the composition NEVER calls navigation.writeEdge itself.

frozen CASCADE_SUBSET, the idempotence guard, and the fable-mode critique seam are CONSUMED from the
