<objective>
Build the outer batch orchestrator (seam e) - the one genuine net-new build. It preflights the plugin load, loops N=200 submissions through a bounded concurrency pool calling `runOne`, checkpoints to a ledger, resumes cleanly, retries transient failures, enforces batch-level guardrails, aggregates the cohort report, and cleans up scratch rooms.

Purpose: N=200 orchestration with per-submission isolation and a clean cost line. The orchestrator NEVER imports runChain - the chain runs inside each spawned session. Two triggers escalate from unit-block to whole-batch halt because they mean the MECHANISM is broken: a Part 8 hit (G3) and a model-provenance mismatch (G4). The batch workspace lives OUTSIDE the repo and is never committed.

Output: `scripts/huji-batch.cjs` exporting `runBatch`.
