<objective>
Measure the movement thresholds on a real portfolio run and record what was measured and on which run (D-04).

Purpose: D-03 fixed the rule (a band crossing, OR a tail entrant, OR a top-N reshuffle). D-04 says the thresholds, meaning N and what counts as a reshuffle, are not guessed. Band crossing and tail entry use definitions that already exist (the band list and the tail classifier), so they need no number. Only the reshuffle needs N, a minimum count of pairs that entered or left the top N, and a minimum rank shift. Part 12 sets the bar: a reshuffle that fires on ordinary wobble reads as noise.
Output: a calibration harness, its tests, the measured thresholds file, and 314-CALIBRATION.md.

Accepted degrade (stated here so nobody reads it as a bug): if the measured numbers cannot separate ordinary growth from a real change, the thresholds file stays calibrated false with a reason, and that is an accepted outcome. What the user sees then: Larry and the command still report pairs that crossed a level and new quiet signals; a change in the order of the top of the list, on its own, triggers no offer, and the command prints the one plain line "Changes in the order of the top of the list are not tracked yet." instead of staying silent. Nothing breaks and no number is invented. The navigator signs off on this in the SUMMARY.

How growth is simulated: a navigator's real change is new content arriving. So "previous" is the room with the newest p percent of things held out (and, for variance, a seeded random p percent held out), and "current" is the full room. The held-out share p is 1, 2, 5 and 10 percent. One percent is ordinary growth the sensor must stay quiet about. Ten percent is a real change it should announce.
</objective>
