<objective>
Give the doctor the honest counts of design section 7 (G3): what the catch-up runner left behind, what the card spool still holds, and whether the last run finished or died.

Purpose: G3: "Honest status: a room status file and doctor counts show what is behind and why." A run killed by a host or a backstop leaves state running; only the doctor can tell that the runner process is dead.

Planner finding (record in the SUMMARY and in the checkpoint): design 7 says the check warns on catchup_state 3 or 5 or a spool file older than 24 h. The target module, room-graph-integrity, is pinned never-warn (its header contract, SEED-074 hard guard, and four `assert.equal(r.status, 'ok')` arms in tests/test-343-room-graph-integrity.cjs). The Goldfish rounds did not record this conflict. Task 1 adds the fields with the contract kept; Task 2 asks the owner where the warn goes; Task 3 builds it when the owner picks the separate module.

Output: the five fields and measureCatchup, test arms for the fields, the AC5 doctor clause and AC6 in tests/test-367-catchup-mcp.cjs, and (by owner choice) a small warn module.
</objective>
