The D-14 appeal gate as a conditional checkpoint after the live build. First establish whether a ruling is needed at all: after a successful build, run every chain suite with the shipped ledger present. If nothing is red and the build did not stop, record that the gate was not triggered and finish. Otherwise put each item in front of the navigator, apply his rulings as data, and rebuild from the recorded raw scores.

Purpose: SPEC R4 (fail loudly, never ship a bad threshold), R5 (runtime integration must not silently break Larry's chains), D-14 final (Jev judges, the navigator audits; a chain-run false alarm fails the build for a ruling).
Output: either a recorded "not triggered", or rulings in the answer key plus a rebuilt ledger and follow-up entries.
</objective>
