   `~/MindrianRooms/rethinking-mindrianos/mindrianOS/` DOES NOT EXIST on disk. The call locked here:
   file to `research/<dated-entry>/` only, and record the mandate-versus-disk discrepancy as a
   finding in the SUMMARY. Do not invent an unprecedented directory to satisfy a doc that disk does
   not support.

only says "per the room's research" without the citation, both count as incomplete work.
