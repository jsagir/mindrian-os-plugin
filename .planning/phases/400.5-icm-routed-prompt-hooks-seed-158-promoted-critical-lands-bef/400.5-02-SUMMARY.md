## The busy-cap decision and its cost

Option (a), one shared cap, is built: the CLI calls setHookBusyCap(HOOK_BUSY_MS) once, so one turn has at most one capped room.db wait (about 750 ms; CITED, plan context). Cost: after a trip, the later room.db checks of the same turn wait 0 ms, and a write that meets a lock is dropped. Each such check is named on stderr with room_db_no_wait <id>; the check that trips the cap is named with room_db_busy_trip <id>. The checks keep their own room_db_busy lines.
