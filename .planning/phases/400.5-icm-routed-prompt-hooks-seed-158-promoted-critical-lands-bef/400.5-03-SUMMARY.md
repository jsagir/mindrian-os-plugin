## Known limits (from the plan context, not in scope)

- After a cut, the engine can still write room.db memory events (reach_presented and gate_reached through logMemoryEvent; Goldfish R4-04) and the cue record (persistFromDecision). The window is bounded by the dispatcher process exit at about 2,500 ms. CITED.
- The zero-score gate marker, the binding gate marker and the no-match marker are written in main() before the engine. They are not late writes and are not guarded. CITED.
- A cut engine leaves no engine trace entry for its turn. That is the same as a host kill today (T-400.5-22, accepted). CITED.
- The check is at the two write sites only. An engine that passes the deadline after persistDecisionTrace and before writeNavBlockHash skips only the hash. That leaves a trace entry with no hash; the next turn then emits a full block (fail open). ASSUMED safe by the T-251-01 fail-open rule.
- L2 proves the cut with deadlineAt = now. A deadline that passes during the engine work is covered by the place of the check (after the engine await), not by a separate leg. UNVERIFIED by test.
