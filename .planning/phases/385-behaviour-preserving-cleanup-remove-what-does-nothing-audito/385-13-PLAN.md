Unwire the per-turn doctor poll Stop hook (PRD scope item 4, auditor T05, owner ruling R1 of 2026-11-06). One finding, one commit. The script stays.

Owner ruling (card answer, 2026-11-06, relayed by the main session): "R1 unwire the per-turn doctor poll Stop hook (script stays)".

Purpose: every Stop with an active room starts a Node process. That process starts a child `node scripts/doctor.cjs --ui-compliance --json` (about 4,700 lines loaded, 5 s timeout) and writes `<room>/.mindrian/last-hmi-poll.json`. Removing the entry saves one process and one doctor child per Stop and changes no output. The ui-compliance signal still comes from `doctor --ui-compliance`.
Output: one hook entry removed, three behaviours added to the plan 385-08 test file, one pinned test and one doc line updated.
