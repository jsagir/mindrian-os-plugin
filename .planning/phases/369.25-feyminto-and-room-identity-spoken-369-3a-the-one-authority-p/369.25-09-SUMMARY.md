## What was built

- `lib/core/graph-self-heal.cjs` detectUnsentineledArtifactFolder: skips a child folder whose name is a key of `CORE_SECTIONS`, a key of `EXTENDED_SECTION_META`, a member of `STRUCTURAL_DIRS` (all three from section-registry.cjs), or a key of `IDENTITY_DIRECTORIES` (room-skeleton-scaffold.cjs, lazily required: it supplies `assets`, which no section-registry export lists), and counts only files for which scaffold-predicate `isScaffoldFile` is false. The three section-registry exports and IDENTITY_DIRECTORIES are the exports used.
- `lib/core/graph-backfill.cjs` runDeriveBackfill step 0a: `readRoomIdentity(root, {door:'in_place'})`; the record goes in `result.readiness`. Without approvedBy a room with no room.db (and a `.room-root`) returns before anything that could open or create room.db. With approvedBy: `repairRoomIdentity` (createDb, moveAsideUnreadable, checkRegistry false so the repair does not judge the registry before the entry exists), `scripts/room-registry create` when the registry lacks the slug, `projectRoomIdentity`, the artifacts under the sections the indexer itself discovers (same predicate and levels as rebuildGraph, scaffold files excluded) through `graph-ops.indexArtifact`, a readback through the owner API, then the normal backfill continues with the internal destructive rebuild skipped (the artifacts were just indexed).
- `stopAfterReadiness:true` records readiness and returns, writing nothing, even under approvedBy (it is the check, not the recovery).

## Decisions and deviations

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug in the plan's rule] The unapproved early return is narrowed to a room with no room.db.** The plan says every not-ready room returns early without approvedBy. Measured: with that rule, two pre-existing tests broke (test-236-backfill-default-preserves-journal 7 to 5 pass lines and exit 1; test-233-drain-backfill-producer-parity 16 to 9 and exit 1), because legacy fixtures hold a room.db (one made only by lazygraph, with no identity table, which readRoomIdentity reports as `room_db_unreadable: no identity table`) and no room.* identity, and they derive without approvedBy. The net now stops unapproved only on `room_db_missing` on a folder that carries `.room-root`, which is the never-ready room and the case where a derive pass would create room.db lazily (Phase 0 R3). A room.db with no identity records its readiness and continues unchanged; under approvedBy any not-ready room (db present or not) is recovered. Plan 13's callers are unaffected: session start uses stopAfterReadiness, the gate calls with approvedBy.
- **2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Return type.** The artifact indexer is async, so an approved recovery that repaired the record and goes on to index returns a Promise even with a synchronous deriveFn. A room whose sources name no slug cannot be registered and cannot become ready, so there the recovery stops after the repair and the call stays synchronous (this keeps test-derive-backfill-acceptance, which calls synchronously with approvedBy on a `{slug}`-only sentinel room, at 4 of 4). Documented in the runDeriveBackfill jsdoc. Plan 13's gate awaits.
- **3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] check-substrate.** A first draft that drove lazygraph-ops directly added a second `require('./lazygraph-ops.cjs')` and the pre-commit net-new gate (`check-substrate.cjs --diff`) refused the commit. The recovery now indexes through the sanctioned `graph-ops.indexArtifact` facade; no allow-list edit.
- **4. Test-side choices.** N7's ready-room hash ignores `room.db-wal` and `room.db-shm`: measured, the first read-only open of a WAL room.db makes SQLite create a 0-byte wal and a 32768-byte shm; room.db itself stays in the hash. N4 brings a born room to ready through the owner repair only when birth did not write the identity (plan 07 landed during this plan, so it usually did); either way the arm measures the net.
- **5. Registry venture name.** `room-registry create` is given the slug as the venture name (nothing invented); the entry also receives the room_id projection.
- **6. State stays the finding.** After a recovery `readiness.state` is still `not_ready` with the original reason; `recovered:true` is the outcome. Plan 13 should read `recovered`.
