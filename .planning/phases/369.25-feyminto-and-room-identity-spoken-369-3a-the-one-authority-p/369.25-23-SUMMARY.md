## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 3 - Blocking] Negative rooms are built under the run's scratch directory, not in the rooms home.** `buildNeverReadyRoom` refuses any target under `~/.mindrian`, which is the real run's default rooms home (`<receipt-dir>/rooms`). Building there would have thrown on the navigator's real run. The leg accepts `roomsHome` for the call shape and writes nothing to it; the negative rooms live in the run's mkdtemp scratch (removed at the end) and never enter the rooms home or its registry (NL2 and NL5 assert both). Cost: "beside the fixture room" is logical, not physical.
2. **[Rule 1 - Bug in plan] Offline FeyMinto leg writes nothing.** The plan has `feymintoLeg` call `deriveSection(..., {offline})` per nest. Offline that rewrote every face from `at_birth` to `offline`, which made test-vi3-icm-walk I8c fail (it pins at_birth for the room real-room-run births). Offline now sends nothing and writes nothing: an already-asked nest keeps its recorded answer and every other nest is reported for this run as "not asked: this run was offline" while the face on disk stays as birth left it. Live runs still derive per nest (handles only).
