## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] lib/mcp/tools/gate.cjs edited (not in the plan's files_modified), and the N12 pin re-pinned.** The plan needs `gate_answer approve` on the recovery card to work for a room with no usable room.db, but gate_answer (a) calls resolveMcpWriteRoom, which now refuses that very room, (b) would write a ratification memory_event and decision node into room.db before running resumeFn (it would create the file the card exists to recover, or fail with no_room_db on a damaged one), and (c) the ledger observer would record a gate_raised row into the room at mint. Two edits: `_onGateMinted` returns early for `entry.recovery === true`, and a new `_answerRecoveryGate` runs right after the answer is normalized (so session, chosen-in-card, verdict-vs-approving, resume owner and card_pending checks all still ran). It requires the session to still be bound to the room the card was drawn for (else `room_switched`, gate left open), takes the gate (single use) and runs resumeFn, returning `chain_result`. The approval is the `approvedBy: gate:<id>` handle the recovery receives; no decision node or memory_event is written (response says `memory_event: null, room_write: skipped_room_record_recovery`). tests/test-365-never-do-gate.cjs pins gate.cjs byte-for-byte to GATE_BASE with a documented re-pin convention; I followed it in a separate commit (bda099f53) with dated comments, descriptions and schemas untouched.
**2. [Rule 1 - Bug in the plan's rule] room_db_missing blocks governed_write and research only for a folder that carries .room-root.** As the plan words it, every room.db-less folder is refused. Measured: test-365-cr01-section-live went red (S1-S3, every chain step into a plain temp folder with a `.mindrian/` and no room.db returned room_not_ready) and test-research-run-session-binding R6c went red (an empty cwd now answered room_not_ready instead of its tool's no_bound_room). ## Known limitations

- The recovery card is minted in the process ledger and leaves no gate_raised record (it cannot, the room has no database), so gate_list and the browser shell do not show it; it reaches the person through the refusal's `recovery_card` (header, notice, options) and `recovery_gate_id`, which the model must present. For a legacy room it is on `room_identity.recovery_gate_id` of a successful result, with no card body in that result. Plan 20 (context_assemble) could carry the card body.
- A recovery approval leaves no decision node: the only record is the approvedBy handle the heal receives (registry, identity and index changes are the durable trace).
- When the session has no identity (a session-less web-transport call) no card is minted; the refusal message points to `/mos:graph --derive`.
- research, feyminto_face and status are answered by readinessFor but only governed_write is wired to a caller in this plan; wiring the research verbs and the face belongs to the plans that own them (FCLOSE-07 is plan 23's negative leg against this definition).
