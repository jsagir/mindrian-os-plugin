## What changed

**Task 1.** `previousMintoSnapshot` gains a `counter` list (null when the previous face has no Counterevidence section, so a pre-plan-15 face cannot make everything look new); it strips the ` [answered: ...]` suffix and the ` (contradicts: ...)` tail and skips the "(model narrative)" and "and N more in the room graph" lines. `updateFeynmanFace` compares the rendered counter records (first RECORD_ROWS_MAX = 8, since the previous face holds no more) by exact trimmed text, pushes at most 3 `Counterevidence added: <text>` lines and `(and N more)` beyond. The diff touches only those two functions. F7 files a CONTRADICTS pair after a first MINTO write, asserts exactly one such line naming the new item, then a third write with no change reads "No change since the previous revision." and carries no such line.

**Task 2.** `maskGeneratedFeynmanLines` removes the frontmatter keys `timeline_last_rendered` and `dial_memory_last_rendered` (inside the frontmatter block only) and the two dial-memory `*Last refreshed: <ISO>. ...*` sentences. The timeline renderer's summary line carries real content (event count, first and last touched times) alongside the refresh time, so for that shape only the refresh time and the relative "(N minutes ago)" text are masked; a changed event count or touched time still moves the digest. BR11 runs the two shipped renderers (timeline-runner and dial-memory-runner `refreshSection`) at one time and an hour later, asserts only the generated lines moved, asserts both `fingerprint` and `full_fingerprint` hold, then appends a narrative sentence and asserts both move; it also unit-checks the masking (event count counts, touched time counts, prose that merely mentions the key name is not masked).
