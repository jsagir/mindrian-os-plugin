## 2. Decisions this phase took where the navigator did not rule

Each is a choice made inside the plan's discretion; the plan and file are named so a reviewer can find it.

- **Instruction-only is defined as** a command that exists on this install but, on this surface, returns reference text only (an internal surface, a missing command file, an MCP tool that is unrouted or in the unimplemented set). A command's file is `commands/<name>.md` or any `commands/*.md` whose frontmatter `name:` is the command (`validate-proposition` lives in `value-proposition.md`) (plan 06).
- **The shared premise is defined as** two or more neighborhood frameworks that share a chapter id; it is rendered as one line of reasoning and the word corroboration is scrubbed from every returned string (plan 08).

## 3. Research trail for the rethinking room

(The orchestrator mirrors this section into rethinking-mindrianos and MindrianOS/research; the executor writes nothing under ~/MindrianRooms.)

### What this phase set out to prove

The 2026-10-05 brief said: prove one authority on the smallest thing that has one, then let the rest consume it. The smallest thing was a room's identity. Before this phase a room was known by its folder name, and nothing could tell a room that had a graph from a room whose graph had been deleted and quietly rebuilt empty. The two field failures behind it (both beta.61 receipts said every search ran, with no rows and no complaint, in a room that had no graph) were the same shape: the work went ahead in a room that could not hold it.

### What was built, in plain words

- A room's identity (its id, name, path, parent, depth, creation time and birth version) is written into the room's own database in the one step that creates the room, and read back on a fresh connection before the room is called ready. ROOM.md, the hidden marker and the registry carry copies and are rewritten from it. A room that cannot hold a record is refused with the reason in plain words; the recoverable cases offer a card named for the job ("Recover this room's record so work can continue"), and only the person's approval runs the repair. A room whose graph was deleted and silently recreated empty is refused as `room_graph_lost`.
- Creating, binding and opening a room all add it to the session; only an explicit unbind removes it; a binding that could not be saved says so.
- FeyMinto is the name of each folder's thinking layer. Its three faces (MINTO, FEYNMAN, BRAIN) are tied to the room id, say which field a person may edit, and the MINTO and FEYNMAN faces gained the missing blocks (counterevidence, assumptions, what would change the conclusion; what changed, what we cannot yet explain). The BRAIN face is the Theo face: it asks Theo per folder with generic handles only, shows how it asked, and says "not asked" with one of six reasons when it did not.
- A generated BRIEF.md in ten blocks per folder (rewritten only when what it rests on changed); session start and the context tool inject the first three blocks (the folder's job, what we think now, the question that matters now) under a fixed size; the next move is composed in one place and every footer points at it.
- The release rule gained a negative leg: three broken rooms, four research jobs, twelve refusals, and the cut is refused without it.

### What was measured

- The walk: section 1 above (identity in room.db no to yes, edit-surface marker 12 of 12 absent to 1 of 32, duplications 1 of 3 to 0 of 3, nests with a brief 0 to 11, every face 0 to 9).
- The design v2 practical test, scripted (`tests/test-36925-practical.cjs`): the job, the argument, the weakest assumption, the proposed move and a newly filed contradiction (in BUT, THE QUESTION THAT MATTERS NOW, WHAT CHANGED and the record basis) all read from BRIEF.md text only; the first run failed on WHAT CHANGED and on a brief going stale after a session start, and both were closed by a gap plan (26).
- Larry's leg B (a born room, its graph deleted, then a silent re-open that makes an empty graph) was reproduced on this tree before plan 27 was written, and plan 27 closed it; it is now one of the three rooms in the negative leg.

### What it taught

- A readback is only as good as the reader: the identity check that said "yes" because a Room node existed (a substring count) was replaced by a read of the seven keys by a different door than the writer's.
- "Legacy" cannot be a catch-all: the exemption that let old rooms keep working also let a re-minted empty graph through, until the evidence on disk (the id recorded outside room.db) told them apart.
- A face that says "(no signal)" for a question that was never asked is a lie by omission; the closed set of "not asked" reasons is the fix.
- Planning for the unhappy path first (each failure test was written red and shown to fail for the real reason) found two plan rules that were wrong against measurement (the early-return rule in the heal net and room_db_missing blocking plain folders), each narrowed by a measured counterexample, and one gap the plans had not seen (the legacy exemption letting a re-minted empty graph through), which Larry's leg B found and plan 27 closed.
