## Decisions and interpretations

- **One function, two triggers.** `renderBriefForSection(roomDir, sectionDir)` calls `renderBrief` and lets it compose the next move for both surfaces through `nextMoveForSection` (the path decide() and suggest_next use). It compares `record_basis_full_fingerprint` (not the input-only one, per plan 18's note, so a new claim or run record re-renders), writes by `BRIEF.md.tmp.<pid>` plus rename, and returns `{ written, skipped, reason }`. It never throws.
- **YOUR DECISION is kept verbatim.** The helper reads the text between the sentinels from the existing file and passes it as `yourDecision` (BW4 proves a planted sentence survives a re-render triggered by a new input). A person's edit outside the sentinels is regenerated away, as the file's header says.
- **Which nests get a brief on stop**: a directory with a ROOM.md and at least one of MINTO.md, FEYNMAN.md, BRAIN.md. A directory with none (assets, references, team in a born room) gets none; BW2 asserts that. This is my reading of "every nest"; the brief reads those three faces.
- **Deadline semantics.** The deadline is checked after each nest, so the first nest always renders and later ones wait for the next stop. Nests with no BRIEF.md go first, then the oldest BRIEF.md by mtime, so a short budget still reaches every nest over successive stops instead of starving the later ones. Skipping a nest costs a render (the fingerprint needs the claim and run digests), measured at about 7 ms per nest.
- **Observability.** One line per stop is appended to `.mindrian/session-close.log` (`on-stop-brief written= skipped= failed= left= elapsed_ms=`), beside the existing phase 88 line.
- **Not in the legacy bare-shell path.** `generateAllSectionMintos` (the no-flag path) does not render briefs; the plan names `--write` and the on-stop walk.

## Findings (not fixed here)

1. **`lib/vault/room-scanner.cjs` KNOWN_SECTIONS is older than the born room.** A born room carries funding and strategy; the scanner's list has `meetings` and not those two, so `generator --write --section funding` exits 2 (`section not found in room`) and those nests never get a MINTO.md or a generator-triggered brief. Their brief comes from the on-stop trigger only. This is why the every-face count is 9 and not the plan's 11. A later plan or quick task should reconcile the list (it is a pre-existing gap, outside this plan's files).
2. **The ROOM.md recompile in `scripts/on-stop` races its own 0.4 s timeout** (`timeout 0.4 node recompile-room-references.cjs`, 14 in parallel). On a fresh room measured here, three or four nests' ROOM.md hashes were still moving at stop 2 (competitive-analysis and strategy at stop 3), and it varies by load. It predates this plan. ROOM.md is a brief input, so a brief legitimately re-renders when its ROOM.md moves; the tests therefore settle first and assert the no-change case from there.
3. **The hook has no room for the full ceiling.** Without this plan the hook already takes about 2.27 s of its 3 s timeout. The 1200 ms default deadline is the guardian precedent the plan named, but 2.27 s plus 1.2 s would exceed 3 s; it does not bind in practice because the real render phase is about 80 ms for 11 nests. If a room had many nests with large records, the ceiling alone would not protect the hook. I kept the plan's default; lowering it is a one-line change.
4. **`lib/memory/decision-capture.test.cjs` Test 12 fails (13/14)**: `archive partition should exist` under `decision-archive/2026-04`, a date-driven archive test unrelated to this plan (it loads none of the files I changed). Logged in `deferred-items.md`; not touched.
