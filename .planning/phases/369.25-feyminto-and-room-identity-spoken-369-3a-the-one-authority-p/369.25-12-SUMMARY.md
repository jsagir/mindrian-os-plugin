## Decisions and deviations

**1. [Rule 2 - missing critical functionality] guardian session-start did not enqueue for room-scope violations (scripts/feynman-minto-guardian.cjs, outside the plan's file list).** The plan's must-have says a critical FEYNMINTO-11 with regenerate_face is enqueued "by the guardian's existing session-start path". Reading runSessionStart showed the enqueue gate looks only at a SECTION result's severity (`result.severity === 'critical'`), and the new validator reports at room scope; arm V9 failed with `got []` after the validator existed, which measured it. Fix: after `validateRoomScope`, each critical violation with `action_hint === 'regenerate_face'` and a section name enqueues that section once, reason `guardian:identity-repair`; the `__room__` entry records `action_taken: enqueued_regen`. A not-ready identity carries repair_room_identity, so it enqueues nothing (V9 checks a stripped room: 0 identity-repair entries). No other plan edits this script (checked each plan's files_modified). Guardian suite still 18/18.

**2. [Rule 1 - bug] the atomic writers validate a tmp name, not MINTO.md.** decision-capture.cjs and vault-section-minto-generator.cjs call `validate()` on `<MINTO.md>.tmp.<pid>.minto`. Keying the 2000 cap only on the basename `MINTO.md` (as the plan states) would leave the writers at 1500 while the guardian allows 2000, so a MINTO between 1500 and 2000 tokens could never be written. The MINTO check matches `^MINTO\.md(\.tmp\..*\.minto)?$`. Other basenames (eureka drafts, filing checks) are unchanged; V6 proves the draft case.

**3. [Rule 1 - bug] heal-command T5's premise moved with the cap (lib/memory/heal-command.test.cjs, outside the plan's list).** The plan expected T5 to keep matching a budget block; at 2000 tokens its 60-artifact mega fixture renders 1967 tokens and passes, so T5 started failing one assertion earlier than its pre-existing `11 !== 10`. Re-pinned the fixture to 80 artifacts with a MOVING comment naming the measurement; T5 now fails only at the old step-count pin, the same signature as at HEAD.

**4. [Process] the plan said to edit "the FEYNMINTO-01-on-MINTO arm" in lib/memory/feynman-minto-invariants.test.cjs; no such arm exists.** Test 8 builds a file named `bloated.md` (neither face), so it keeps the 1500 arm and I left it, adding a comment. Tests 23 (MINTO.md at 1600 and 2000 pass, 2001 is FEYNMINTO-12), 24 (FEYNMAN budget at 1500 and 1501) and 25 (INVARIANT_IDS, CATEGORIES.IDENTITY) were added. My RED commit carried a one-byte fixture error in Test 23 (an extra newline made the 2000-token case 2001); the fix is in the GREEN commit.

**5. [Fixture note] V4 keys the faces itself.** A born room's nests hold a FEYNMAN.md with no `room_id`, and the validator reports each as a warning (`face not yet keyed to the room id`) until plan 17 stamps it at birth. So V4's "zero violations after repair" step rewrites each FEYNMAN.md and MINTO.md with the repaired room_id first; V3 asserts no critical and no field-`room` violations rather than none at all.

Not changed: the FEYNMINTO label on MINTO budget messages keeps the words `budget exceeded`, which heal-command step 7 matches (`/budget exceeded/i`), so the heal block still records for a section over 2000 tokens.
