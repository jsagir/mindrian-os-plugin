The FEYNMAN what-changed list is written by `updateFeynmanFace` in scripts/vault-section-minto-generator.cjs (plan 369.25-15). It does appear elsewhere in the brief (BUT Counterevidence, THE QUESTION, and FEYNMAN's cannot-explain list as "Counterevidence with no answer yet"), so the brief does show what to reconsider; what it lacks is the line in WHAT CHANGED that tells a returning navigator it arrived since last time. The plan's instruction for a failing arm is to stop and report the owning plan, so P5b is a hard assertion and stays red. Suggested fix, about 8 lines in `updateFeynmanFace`: parse the previous MINTO's `## Counterevidence` bullets in `previousMintoSnapshot`, and push `Counterevidence added: <text>` into `changed` for each `records.counter` item whose text was not in them (and the same for assumptions if the navigator wants it).
