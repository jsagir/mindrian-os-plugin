Decisions this plan fixes:
- The brief's size contract is 2000 tokens of body (bytes / 4): with MINTO at 2000 (FEYNMINTO-12), FEYNMAN at 1500 (FEYNMINTO-01) and the largest measured fixture load of 1902 tokens, the walk's per-nest load stays at 7402 of its 8000 flag (derivation in plan 12).
- Annex C16 names the five labels but does not define them (369.2-LARRY-ANNEX.md:137-141: "distinguish quote_matched, source_reports, derived, independently_checked, and canonically_confirmed. Render the actual state beside each claim."). The definitions below are written from those names and Canon Part 9 ("only a human confirms a truth-claim node") and ship as data the navigator can amend: quote_matched: the quoted words were found verbatim in the named source; it says nothing about whether the source is right; record: the source and the matched passage. source_reports: a source says this and nobody here has checked it beyond reading it; record: the source. derived: reasoned here from other recorded claims; it is only as strong as those claims; record: the input claim ids. independently_checked: a second, independent source or a run result agrees; record: the second source or the verification record. canonically_confirmed: a person confirmed this claim in room.db (Canon Part 9); record: the confirmed node and who confirmed it.
- The claim-state resolver reads room records only through lib/core/navigation.cjs readers (readClaimVerification, readVerificationPortrait), never a direct db open.
- Attempted records: `.mindrian/research-runs/<id>/run.json` read-only (its status fields as written at lib/core/research-planner/quick.cjs:876 and deep.cjs:1314; the executor names them in the SUMMARY); a run with no terminal status, or a shape not recognized, renders "unresolved".
