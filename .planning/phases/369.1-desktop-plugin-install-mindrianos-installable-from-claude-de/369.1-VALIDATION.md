- **Before `/gsd-verify-work`:** the floor (exit 0, FAILED=0, KNOWN=4, the three live legs RAN and PASSED) plus `bash tests/run-all-266.sh`, `bash tests/run-all-341.sh`, `bash tests/run-all-267.sh`, `node scripts/build-connector-registry.cjs --check`, `node scripts/check-render-coverage.cjs`, `node scripts/doctor.cjs --acceptance`, `bash scripts/verify-release`
