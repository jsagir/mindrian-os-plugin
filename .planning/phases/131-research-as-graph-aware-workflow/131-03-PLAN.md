  HSI-scoring of findings is DEFERRED to the v1.14.0 fan-out behind Phase 134's CJS HSI.

  activated HERE (the engine's source slot goes from reserved client_count 0 to active).

  [{ lens, weight }] tuples) produced by Plan 02's computeLensSet. It does NOT recompute,
  re-derive, or re-weight the lenses -- the 4th source-family weighting is OWNED by Plan 02.
  This driver only ORDERS its rotation by the supplied descending weight (weighted =
  ordered-serial by the given weight) and must not drop or override any supplied weight.

ranked findings; the command (Plan 05) orchestrates the driver after the extractor.

(already registered in run-all-131.sh by Plan 01; this plan only CREATES the suite file).
