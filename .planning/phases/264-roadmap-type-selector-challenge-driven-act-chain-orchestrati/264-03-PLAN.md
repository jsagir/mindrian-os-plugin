## Decisions resolved in this plan

- **RESEARCH Open Question 1 / Assumption A3 (which `reach_id`)**: resolved to `context_block`.
  `brain_consult` and `deep_research` are suppressed by `isReachEligibleForTurn` when
  `turnCount < 3`, and a navigator states a research goal in the first turn or two, which is
  exactly the dead zone. `context_block` is never turn-stage-suppressed and is what 12 of 19
  shipped sensors already fire. No seventh reach is minted.
- **RESEARCH Assumption A2 (`SENS_PRIORITY` group)**: resolved to Group C, as the LAST member,
  immediately after `'SENS-SHOW',` and immediately before the `// Group D` comment. Group D is
  documented in the file as holding "the only pure FALLBACK-tier member" (SENS-16); this
  sensor is not pure-fallback because its cascade fires on signal and context tiers first.
  Both placements satisfy Canon Part 11 R3; Group C avoids amending SENS-16's claim.
- **Tie-break policy (planner's call, D-02/D-06 territory)**: ties in the keyword tier are
  broken by `ROADMAP_TYPES` declaration order and the fact is recorded as
  `evidence.tie_broken = true`, so a tie is observable rather than a silent coin flip. The
  sensor still fires (D-06's single-hit doctrine), it does not go silent on a tie.
