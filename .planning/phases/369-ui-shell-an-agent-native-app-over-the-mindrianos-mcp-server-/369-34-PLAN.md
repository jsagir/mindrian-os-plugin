<objective>
Find out why journey step 6 sometimes never sees the read copy converge after both servers restart, prove it, and fix it at its cause; and close REV369-12 (the relay poll that ignores a reset sequence or epoch), which lives in the same read-copy path.

Purpose: gap 3 of 369-VERIFICATION.md. The close gate saw 1 failing run in 10 ("timeout waiting for the read copy to hold all 19 items of room-x", 60 s, the page on Work saying "Nothing changed since your last visit"); load at the moment was not captured. 369-23 recorded that restart convergence is dominated by the relay reconnect backoff (1 s, 2 s, 4 s) with the 2 s poll as the net, and the review (WR-10) found that the poll only emits on a HIGHER sequence. The orchestrator's rule: establish the cause with evidence (the relay backoff, host load, or a real race) and fix or harden with a stated reason; never just raise the timeout.

Hypotheses the evidence must decide between (none is assumed):
- H1 the room grew after the test captured its expected count: step 6 snapshots `expected = total(truth(roomX))` before the shell reopens the room; a bookkeeping row written after that (for example a bind or derivation memory event whose 60 s logEvent dedupe window has passed, which happens only on a slow run) makes the copy hold more items than `expected`, so `count === expected` can never be true while the copy is in fact correct.
- H2 the copy is behind: no hint reached the page after the restart (stream in backoff, the relay's first poll records the head without emitting), so no pull ran.
- H3 the read copy is stuck in a non-current state after the restart (ReplicaProvider's open or retry path).
- H4 host load alone.

Output: evidence capture and a soak mode in the journey, the RCA record, the fix at the proven cause, the WR-10 relay fix with a unit arm, and a rebuilt dist.
</objective>
