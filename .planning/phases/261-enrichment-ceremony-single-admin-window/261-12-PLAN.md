**Sizing note, stated deliberately, in the same spirit as plan 258-06's.** This plan carries EIGHT
tasks rather than the usual two or three, and four of them are blocking checkpoints. That is a
considered override, not an oversight, and the reasoning is the same one 258-06 recorded: splitting

large to review properly at its own checkpoint is its own failure mode. The mitigation is that the
approval is split across FOUR checkpoints grouped by DECISION HOMOGENEITY rather than one
mega-approval, per `.planning/research/FEATURES.md`'s batch-ceremony design: classification rulings
on one card, mechanical payload waves on the next, source-authority judgments on the third, graph
hygiene on the fourth. Window integrity beats task-count tidiness; card meaning beats card count.
