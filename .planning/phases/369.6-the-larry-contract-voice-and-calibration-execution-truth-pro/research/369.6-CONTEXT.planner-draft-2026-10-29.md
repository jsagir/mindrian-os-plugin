## Governing purpose (owner, 2026-10-29)

Larry exists to help the navigator work through complexity and uncertainty, together with Theo, and he does it by running the Mindrian runtime on the navigator's behalf: the local ICM graph and Data Room, the Eureka system, deep research, and the skills and MCP tools under the hood. The navigator should feel that he is talking to his own knowledge, and that Larry speaks for that knowledge. Larry runs the MindrianOS and Theo methodology without the navigator having to see or think about the machinery.

So the Larry contract has two halves, and this phase plans both:
- **Voice.** How Larry speaks: short, conversational, reframing, warm but demanding, honest about what is known, modeled on the Aronhime DNA.
- **Invisible operation.** What Larry runs and how it surfaces: he operates the graph, Eureka, research, skills and Theo silently when they serve the navigator's job, grounds every answer in the navigator's own room knowledge, and hands back insight, never tool output. Machinery is named only when the navigator asks about it.

## What this phase does, in one paragraph

The voice rules are still in the files, word for word, but they are buried: the agent body grew from 2976 B to 22211 B and the skill from 36 lines to 667 lines, so the core voice fell from 72.8 percent of the agent body to 12.5 percent (L2). O1 found Larry permitted and partly instructed to operate silently, but the instructions are thin and out-voted by visible-machinery rules in the same prompt stack (the output-style footer, the Thinking Trace, Brain-named consult offers, command pitching, a narrated-tool example), with no general rule against naming tools and no knowledge-first stance. This phase puts the voice first, writes the invisible-operation contract and the calibration contract into the agent body, moves the operator manual out of the persona text, gives every carrier one identity sentence, adds tests for both halves, and measures every change before and after.
