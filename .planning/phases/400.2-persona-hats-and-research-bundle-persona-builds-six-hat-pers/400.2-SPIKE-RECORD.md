## Limits (stated plainly)

- The measure ran keyless: no Tavily key, no PatentsView key, no OpenAlex key. The Tavily industry lens and the patents lens of green were not measured through their own providers.
- The three industry lenses (white, black, yellow) ran as host web searches by the main session with the exact plan strings: white 10 results, black 10, yellow 10. Code cannot prove that the host searched exactly that string.
- Kaggle (white, `site:kaggle.com/datasets`) and AskNature (green, `site:asknature.org`) returned no on-site results through the host search. The two searches returned only off-site links, which were not passed in. Their lens was therefore measured as ran with zero items. The spike says nothing about the overlap those two sources would add.
- A door lens run returns 5 findings or fewer for each call. The sets are small (5 to 10 items), so one shared paper moves a share by 0.1 to 0.2.
- One generic handle was used for all four hats. In real use Green's handle names another field, which should lower the green overlap further. This was not measured.
- One run, one handle, one day. A changed qualifier or lens set needs a new record.
