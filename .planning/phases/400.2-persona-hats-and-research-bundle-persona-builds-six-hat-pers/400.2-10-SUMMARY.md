# Phase 400.2 Plan 10: Part 8 controls on a door search string Summary

The door now has the four checks on a search handle in a fixed order (content fence on the raw text, generic shape, digits, room identity screen) and a composer that adds the fixed hat qualifier, audits the string again and returns its q_hash. Nothing is wired into plan-research yet; plan 11 does that.

## What was built

- **Task 1** (commit 67579cfcb): three frozen constants at the end of the additions block of `lib/core/persona-ops.cjs` (qualifier table, lens table, status `provisional`), exported. `IDENTITY_STOP_LIST` and `roomIdentityScreen(roomDir, readingList)` in the door. The screen reads the venture name with the two rules of `extractDomainSignals`, adds the room directory base name, the base names of files under `team/` (depth 3, cap 200) and the proper-noun candidates of the reading-list files (a capital word that does not start a line or a sentence). Fail-closed: no venture name, or "Unknown Venture", gives `ran: false`.
- **Task 2** (commit a15cd496f): `checkHandle(rawHandle, screen)` with the order fence, shape (equals `genericDomainHandle`, 1 to 4 words), digits, identity screen. Reasons: `egress_violation`, `handle_not_generic_shape`, `handle_has_digit`, `identity_screen_unavailable`, `room_identity_word`. `composeString(hat, handle)` with reasons `hat_never_searches`, `handle_not_generic_shape` and the reason of `auditEditedQuery`; success holds `text`, `handle`, `qualifier`, `q_hash`.
- Frozen files not touched (`git diff bb0bfbab3` on `lib/core/bono/`, `lane-queries.cjs`, `rs-egress-prompts.cjs` is empty). All library modules are required lazily inside the functions.

## Known limits (stated, not defects)

- The proper-noun rule treats an abbreviation such as "Dr." as a sentence end, so the name after it is not screened. A name that the room never states in a screened place is caught by the approval card only (risk K-3).
- A Title-Case heading puts its non-first words into the screened set, so a field word in a heading can be refused (over-refusal is the accepted side, T-4002-48).
