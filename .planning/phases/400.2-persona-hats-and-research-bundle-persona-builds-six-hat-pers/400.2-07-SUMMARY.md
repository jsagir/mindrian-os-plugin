## How the check step decides (as built)

- Order for each of white, red, black, yellow, green: persona not usable (not_run:persona_refused, not_run:persona_build_failed, not_run:persona_not_accepted), no reading file (not_run:no_reading_file), then readJsonFile faults (rejected:schema_invalid:json, rejected:file_too_large, rejected:not_a_regular_file), validateReading (rejected:<reason>), citationCheck (rejected:citation_outside_sources), then rule lines (reading ran).
- Rule lines: the governance strings as returned, plus white_missing_data_absent, red_line_too_long, none_found_with_evidence, none_found_without_search, green_evidence_disposition. Yellow none_found on a valid basis (FS-10) drops yellow_value_not_evidence_backed and sets none_found_basis (door_search_empty or self_reported_search).
- Citation check: evidence sources are mapped to the canonical allowed form (final slash or full stop ignored) and passed to validateCitations; then every string field of the reading (all depths, the url field of a self-found source excepted) is scanned for http://, https://, www. and EvidenceClaim: ids. A www. form matches an allowed URL of either scheme; an http:// or https:// form must match the scheme. Trailing sentence punctuation is ignored in prose.
- The refused text is never echoed: a reason holds a key name only if it is a plain identifier of 24 characters or fewer, else `_`.

## Planner decisions as built

- The schema is lenient where the plan says "rule line, not rejection": evidence, missing_data, nothing_missing, provocations, steelman, none_found, self_search_strings and self_found_sources are all optional keys; a missing or empty one is judged by the rule lines. Evidence items have optional source, tier, disposition, note (a missing source is rejected by the citation check; a missing tier is the white rule line). Black items must carry disposition disconfirming or confirming.
- Blue: skipped, thin, ask_next are optional arrays of 0 to 7 non-empty strings of 200 characters or fewer; each thin entry must contain a hat name. check itself does not read a Blue reading.
- Limits chosen by the executor where the plan gave none: 40 evidence items, 20 missing_data lines, 24-character key names in reasons, state strings in the Blue ledger cut to 60 characters.
- plan-research refuses a second call once run_research exists (step_already_done:run_research) so a later plan cannot lose a real pack by a repeated call.
