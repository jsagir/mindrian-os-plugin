Checks on a handle, in this order (DESIGN 4.5):
1. Content fence on the raw handle: auditQueryString(rawHandle, 'persona-hats') of lib/core/rs-egress-prompts.cjs:72-91 throws on a hit; the door maps it to egress_violation.
2. Shape: the raw handle must equal genericDomainHandle(rawHandle) (lib/core/bono/cell-fanout.cjs:76-80) and have 1 to 4 words; else handle_not_generic_shape. The door never normalises a handle silently.
3. Digits: a handle with a digit is refused with handle_has_digit. No year exception.
4. Room identity screen: room_identity_word, or identity_screen_unavailable when the screen cannot run.
5. Compose: string = handle + one space + HAT_RESEARCH_QUALIFIER[hat].
6. auditEditedQuery(string, {auditFn}) of lib/core/dominant-design/lane-queries.cjs:172-192, with auditFn = auditQueryString on the surface persona-hats.
7. q_hash = qHash(string) of lib/core/research-planner/families.cjs:264.

Room identity screen (DESIGN 8):
- Sources of screened tokens: (a) the venture name of STATE.md, read as extractDomainSignals reads it (the venture: line, else the first heading); (b) the base name of the room directory; (c) the base names of the files under team/; (d) proper-noun candidates of the reading-list files: a token that starts with a capital letter and is not the first token of a sentence or of a heading.
- Match rule: lower-case both sides and split on every character outside a-z and 0-9. A handle is refused when one of its tokens equals a screened token of 4 or more letters that is not in the stop-list, or when the full venture name occurs in the handle as a run of consecutive tokens of any length.
- IDENTITY_STOP_LIST: a frozen list in the door. It holds at least: room, venture, project, company, startup, labs, group, systems, solutions, technologies, global, health, energy, data, market, platform, network, digital, research, design, care, food, water, power, cloud, mobile, smart, green, blue, open, new, test, demo.
- Fail-closed: when the venture name is absent or is "Unknown Venture" (persona-ops.cjs:183), roomIdentityScreen returns {ran: false} and every handle check returns identity_screen_unavailable.
- Counts only leave the screen in a file: {ran, venture_name_found, tokens_checked}. The token set stays in memory.

What each control cannot catch is in the table of DESIGN 8. The card is the final control for a name that no screen knows (risk K-3).
