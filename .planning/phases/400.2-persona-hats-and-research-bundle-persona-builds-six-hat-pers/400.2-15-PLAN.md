Definitions (planner; DESIGN gives the rule and not the formula):
- The URL set of a hat is the set of normalised URLs of the items of its evidence pack.
- share(A, B) = size of the intersection divided by the size of the smaller set. When one set is empty the share is null and the pair is marked no_result.
- decision: pass when every pair with a number has a share of 0.5 or less; change_needed when 1 or more pairs are above 0.5; inconclusive when 3 or more of the 6 pairs are no_result.
- The record holds: date, the handle, the 4 strings, the providers of each hat, the item count of each hat, the 6 shares and the decision. It holds no URL and no title.
- runSourceLens returns 5 findings or fewer for a call, so a hat has 5 URLs or fewer for one string. The record states this limit.

Why the tools live under tests/: package.json ships scripts/ and does not ship tests/. A spike tool must not ship.

The spike uses the real door path, so it also proves the path once against live providers: start, write-persona, accept-persona, plan-research, the approved file, run-research. To need no agent run, the preparation tool writes fixed persona specs and one fixed generic handle for the four hats into the hat directories. The handle is "battery recycling" (DEFAULT CHOSEN by the planner; a generic field term with no tie to any room; the owner may give another handle on the run sheet).
