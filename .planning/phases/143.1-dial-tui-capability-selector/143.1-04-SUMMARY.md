## Decisions Made
- **Plan-named renderer helpers do not exist (Rule 3 blocking issue).** The plan + UI-SPEC reference `applyBrainSuggestionVariant` / `composeBrainOptionRows` / `appendAskUserQuestionTrailer` as `shape-f1-renderer.cjs` exports "per Section 14 reuse map". The shipped renderer exports only `renderShapeF1` + `CANONICAL_VERBS`. `renderShapeF1` IS the Shape F.1 Brain-suggestion variant (its Mode A `▶` marker is the apply-brain-variant behavior; its `verbs` arg is the option-row composer; its returned `contract` with `keyboard: 'askuserquestion'` is the trailer behavior). Reused `renderShapeF1` directly and documented the named-helper mapping in the header so the `key_links` reuse pattern is honored honestly. This satisfies the plan INTENT (reuse the shipped renderer, no bespoke widget) without inventing functions that do not exist.
- **S2 two-marker near-tie:** `renderShapeF1` marks at most one `recommendedVerb`, but S2 needs two. Resolved by glyphing each row with the frozen-gate `recommended` flag from the ReachList core and passing the already-glyphed rows to `renderShapeF1` as `verbs`. The renderer still owns the F.1 contract + numbering + trailer; the core owns marker truth. No MAX_K change, no bespoke widget.
