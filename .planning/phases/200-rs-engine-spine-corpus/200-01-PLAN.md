<objective>
Fix the SEED-018 degenerate-output bug at its two root causes: a polluted corpus
(meta-folders counted as room artifacts, inflating room_count to 706 and swamping
the differential with noise) and an ungated external fetch (OpenAlex keyword matches
enter the unified matrix even when semantically off-topic). This is the self-contained
P2 bug fix - it does not touch the graph writers or the embedding spine, and it is the
highest-value, lowest-risk plan in Phase 200.

Root cause (traced, not guessed): `load_room_artifacts` walks the room tree and counts
every markdown-ish file, including `.heal-backup/`, `.intelligence/`, `.room-graph/`
and the RS engine's own `.rs-engine-results-*.json` output - so the corpus is mostly
machinery, not venture content. The LSA/semantic differential then lands every pair at
the boundary extreme (lsa 1.0 / semantic 0.0) because the two spaces are measuring noise
against noise. The external fetch compounds it: OpenAlex returns are keyword-matched and
never gated on topic similarity, so atmospheric-remote-sensing papers score high on a
"multi-user team collaboration" topic.
</objective>
