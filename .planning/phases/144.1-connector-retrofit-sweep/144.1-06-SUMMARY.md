## Decisions Made
- **logSpineRead is the firing primitive.** The EVENT_TYPES set in lib/core/navigation/memory-events.cjs is frozen and logEvent rejects unknown event_types; minting a new "reach_fired" type was out of scope and unnecessary. spine_read is the generic chokepoint memory_event already exported from navigation.cjs and used by routing/firing surfaces, so the Tier D hooks reuse it (Canon Part 7 reuse-before-build).
- **Bash hooks fire via a shim, not inline node -e.** scripts/fire-sensor-event.cjs centralizes the navigation require + Part-8 payload discipline in one auditable file.
- **No 6th reach / no new sensor.** Every fire uses the frozen reach_ids (context_block, deep_research, brain_consult) and the existing SENS-01/03/04/05/06 ids. NO 6th reach/sensor was minted (per fences).
- **Registry untouched.** Hooks are .cjs/bash scripts, not connector-bearing .md surfaces; the connector-registry generator walks .md only, so no regen + no --check was required (per fences).
