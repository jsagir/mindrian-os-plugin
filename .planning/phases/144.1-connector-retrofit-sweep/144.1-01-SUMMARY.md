### Noted Intent Deviations (not auto-fixes)

**2. Null-framework filing intent (mva-brief, mva-option, opportunities)**
- The audit (items 22, 23, 25) recorded intent of `memory_event_only` (mva-brief, mva-option) and `fileEvidenceWithReadback` (opportunities). These three commands declare NO `frameworks:` block, so their connectors carry `framework: null`. The shipped WFL-01 `--check` (build-connector-registry.cjs lines 424-436) treats `firesCommand` as true whenever `filing != none` OR a decision surface is set, and then requires the framework to resolve via `commandsForFramework()` -- which a null framework cannot. To pass the shipped check WITHOUT fabricating a framework (no invented methodology, per the contract Section 4 degrade-don't-fabricate rule), these three use `filing: none` + no surface. The surfaces still emit their `memory_event` through their existing code paths; only the connector's `filing` field is `none`. This is the only legal null-framework configuration under the shipped tripwire.
