Two states carry an adjustment note: Searching and Found use paper on cobalt for the upper eye. The renderer eases between states over 760 ms; a terminal cannot ease, so the terminal face changes state instantly and never loops (mos-brand book e947a5d, segments/experience/terminal: the Theo face may change between states and never loops for decoration).
