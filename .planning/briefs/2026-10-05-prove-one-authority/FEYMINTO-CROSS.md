## The one idea

The brief's target loop is: workspace defines the job, Theo recommends a method, Mindrian selects an admissible action, the runtime executes and records, the room writers persist, verification controls promotion, the human keeps the consequential decisions. FeyMinto (SEED-122) is that loop at the grain of one folder (one nest of the room). Prove the authority on the room (its identity in `room.db`), then let every nest consume it through FeyMinto, and the brief's "generalize" step has a vehicle instead of a new ontology.

## The orchestration loop, per nest

1. Identity: the room's id from `room.db`; the nest's section kind and lens.
2. Ask: Theo, with handles only; the answer lands in the Theo face with provenance.
3. Resolve: each recommendation marked runnable here / instruction-only / assisted.
4. Choose: the navigator picks on a card named by its job (never "grant", never an id); Larry's next move is read from the face.
5. Execute: the runtime runs the command; the operation ledger records every operation and its terminal state.
6. Persist: the room writers file artifacts, claims, edges; filing waits for its own card.
7. Regenerate: FeyMinto re-renders what ran, what changed, what is next; FEYNMINTO-07 and the invariants validate; the guardian reports.
8. Read back: `readTriple` is what Larry, session-start, the doctor and the real-room run read.
